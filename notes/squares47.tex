\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Journal step 81 named the next lock-picking stroke: certify that the
$47\times47$ matrix of the truncated Weil form at $\mu=11$, assembled
from primes, agrees with the Gram matrix of the zero-evaluators
$\eta\mapsto\hat\eta(\gamma)$. MUSIC already saw the identity as a
fact of subspaces. This note records the exact pairing, ships a
replay script against $\mathtt{zeros280.pkl}$, and states what ball
arithmetic still owes.
\end{abstract}

\section{The identity}

On $V=\mathrm{span}(\eta_0,\dots,\eta_{46})$ at $L=\log11$, the
explicit formula says that the truncated Weil form assembled from the
pole, the archimedean place and the prime towers equals, term by term,
\[
Q(\eta_n,\eta_m)
\;=\;
\sum_{\gamma}| \widehat{\eta_n}(\gamma)|\,|\widehat{\eta_m}(\gamma)|
\cdot\mathrm{sign}_{nm}(\gamma)
\;=\;
\sum_{\gamma}\widehat{\eta_n}(\gamma)\,\widehat{\eta_m}(\gamma),
\]
the $\gamma$ running over the nontrivial zeros of $\zeta$ (even
window: $\gamma$ and $-\gamma$ identified). The Fourier transforms of
the shifted-cosine basis are closed form:
\[
\widehat{\eta_0}(\gamma)
=\frac{2\sin(\gamma L/2)}{\gamma\sqrt L},\qquad
\widehat{\eta_k}(\gamma)
=\sqrt{\frac2L}\,\frac{2\gamma\sin(\gamma L/2)}{\gamma^2-\omega_k^2}
\quad(k\ge1).
\]
Call $Q^{\mathrm{pr}}$ the matrix built from $P+A-\sum_p T_p$ and
$Q^{\mathrm{z}}$ the matrix with entries
$\sum_{\gamma}\widehat{\eta_n}(\gamma)\widehat{\eta_m}(\gamma)$.
The $47$ squares are the diagonal of $Q^{\mathrm{z}}$; the theorem
wanted is $Q^{\mathrm{pr}}=Q^{\mathrm{z}}$ as matrices on $V$, with
certified enclosures.

This is not RH. It is the explicit formula restricted to a
finite-dimensional even window, with the usual archimedean
normalization already checked on $\eta_0$ by
$\mathtt{weil\_normalization\_check.py}$.

\section{What is already seen}

MUSIC~\cite{LabNB} \S16.1 confirmed the identity as a subspace fact:
the prime-assembled matrix and the zero-assembled matrix share the
same radical, recover $\gamma_1$ to $10^{-19}$, and drop at the band
frontier $\omega_{\max}\approx120.5$. That is a measurement of
$Q^{\mathrm{pr}}\approx Q^{\mathrm{z}}$ on the quasi-null flag. It
does not enclose every entry.

The public normalization check certifies two scalar identities
(pole exact, archimedean against a $\psi$-integral to the $1/T$ tail)
on the single vector $\eta_0$. Forty-six more diagonal squares, and
the off-diagonal Gram, remain.

\section{The script}

$\mathtt{code/squares47.py}$ rebuilds $Q^{\mathrm{pr}}$ (mpmath,
same closed $\Theta$ as the quorum engine) and $Q^{\mathrm{z}}$ from
$\mathtt{zeros280.pkl}$, and prints
\[
\max_{n,m\le N_0}\bigl|Q^{\mathrm{pr}}_{nm}-Q^{\mathrm{z}}_{nm}\bigr|
\quad\text{and the relative residual on the diagonal}.
\]
Default $N_0=6$ (cheap). $N_0=46$ is the full $47$ squares and takes
longer. The script does not use flint; promoting the same difference
to Arb balls is the certification stroke, identical in kind to
$\mathtt{positivite\_certifiee.py}$.

\section{What certification must enclose}

Three errors, all explicit:
\begin{enumerate}
\item quadrature / closed-form evaluation of $P$, $A$, $T_p$
(already ball-controlled in the positivity script, radii
$\le10^{-55}$);
\item tail of $\sum_{\gamma>\gamma_{\mathrm{cut}}}\widehat{\eta_n}(\gamma)\widehat{\eta_m}(\gamma)$,
algebraic $O(1/\gamma)$ as measured by MUSIC, hence a bound
$O(\gamma_{\mathrm{cut}}^{-1})$ after summing;
\item conversion of the known zeros to Arb (mpmath $\mathtt{zetazero}$
at working $dps$, or a frozen dyadic file).
\end{enumerate}
Item 2 is the only new estimate. A cut at $\gamma=280$ (the shipped
cache) is enough for a $10^{-6}$ residual on the bulk of $V$ and
should be enough to see the razor $3.58\times10^{-48}$ on the ground
state only after the tail is budgeted, not ignored.

\section{Status}

The identity is the explicit formula on $V$. MUSIC plus the
$\eta_0$ normalization check make it a measurement. The $47$
certified squares are the same identity with radii. The script is the
replay; flint is the certificate.

\section*{Addendum --- tail enclosed, 1 September}

Extended cache \texttt{zeros500.pkl} ($\gamma_{500}=811$). Replay at
$N_0=4$: residual $4.1\%$ (was $6.1\%$ at $\gamma=514$). Explicit
envelope on the remainder
\[
\bigl|Q^{\mathrm{z}}_{nm}-\sum_{|\gamma|\le G}\hat\eta_n(\gamma)\hat\eta_m(\gamma)\bigr|
\;\le\;
2c^2\int_G^\infty\frac{\tfrac1{2\pi}\log(g/2\pi)+1}{g^2}\,dg
\]
with $c=4/\sqrt L$, gives $3.2\times10^{-2}$ at $G=811$. Combined
budget on each entry of the $5\times5$ block:
$|Q^{\mathrm{pr}}-Q^{\mathrm{z}}|\le 4.3\times10^{-3}+3.2\times10^{-2}$.
This is a remainder, not an Arb certificate of equality. The identity
on $V$ is measured at four percent plus an explicit $O(1/G)$ tail.
Flint can take the same difference to balls; the zeros are not yet
Arb objects.
