\begin{abstract}
\noindent
$Q$ converges in three parameters, already measured, with three
different meanings. In the number $K$ of zeros kept, $Q_K\to Q^{\mathrm{pr}}$
in Frobenius, with a drop at the band edge and an algebraic tail
$O(1/\omega_{\max})$. In the basis size $N$, $\lambda_{\min}(Q_N)\downarrow c_L>0$
under RH (the floor). In the window $L$, $Q_L\succeq c_L>0$ for all $L$
is RH. Nothing new is computed here; the rates are named.
\end{abstract}

\section{$K$: the explicit formula as a matrix limit}
On $V_N$ at fixed $\mu$,
\[
Q^{\mathrm{pr}}=P+A-\sum_p T_p,
\qquad
Q_K=\sum_{k\le K}2\,\hat\eta(\gamma_k)\hat\eta(\gamma_k)^{\!\top}.
\]
Measured at $\mu=11$, $N=47$, $\omega_{\max}=120.53$:
\[
\frac{\|Q^{\mathrm{pr}}-Q_K\|_F}{\|Q^{\mathrm{pr}}\|_F}
=
\begin{cases}
31\% & K=35,\;\gamma=112,\\
8.9\% & K=40,\;\gamma=123,\\
5.1\% & K=60,\;\gamma=163.
\end{cases}
\]
The drop sits on the band edge. The remainder is the off-band lobes
$\hat\eta_n(\gamma)\sim 1/(\gamma^2-\omega_n^2)$.
\begin{lemma}[tail]
$\sum_{\gamma>\omega_{\max}}|\hat\eta_n(\gamma)\hat\eta_m(\gamma)|
\le C_N/\omega_{\max}$.
\end{lemma}
An Arb enclosure of $Q^{\mathrm{pr}}-Q^{\mathrm{z}}$ on the whole
$V_{47}$ puts every one of the $1128$ independent entries in a ball
about $0$ of radius $6\times10^{-3}$ (\S65). That is convergence in
$K$ certified, not a density-$1$ residual.

\section{$N$: the floor}
At fixed $L$, $V_N\uparrow PW_{L/2}^{\mathrm{even}}$. Under RH,
$Q$ is the Gram of the evaluators on that space, so
\[
\lambda_{\min}(Q_N)\ \downarrow\ c_L=\inf_{\|f\|_2=1}\sum_{\gamma}|\hat f(\gamma)|^2>0
\]
(Beurling / Duffin--Schaeffer on a set of density $<L/\pi$,
\emph{sampling-floor}). Measured:
at $\mu=3$, $c_{\log3}=5.55\times10^{-8}$, ratios $1.32\to1.02$;
at $\mu=11$, $\lambda_{\min}(N)=3.59,1.86,1.54,1.4\times10^{-48}$ for
$N=47,57,67,80$ --- flattening, not a new decade per ten modes.
The statement ``one keeps digging'' (\S54) is false. $Q_N$ converges
as an operator on each compact of $PW_{L/2}$ to the Beurling form;
only the razor is numerically ugly.

\section{$L$: the one that is RH}
$Q_L$ on $PW_{L/2}$, $L\to\infty$, asks for a uniform $c_L>0$.
Under RH this is the sampling floor with more and more points; the
constant is expected to decay (geometric law, desert).
Unconditionally it is Weil positivity at every compact window, i.e.\
RH. There is no measured limit $Q_L\to Q_\infty$ in the repository
that would bypass that.

\section*{Status}
$K$ converges, with a named tail and an enclosure. $N$ converges,
to a positive floor under RH, measured at two windows. $L$ does not
converge in the deposit; that limit is the theorem. Not a new factor
of $Q$. Not RH.
