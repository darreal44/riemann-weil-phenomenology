\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Windowed Weil positivity is a sampling inequality for the zeros of $\zeta$
in the Paley--Wiener space $PW_{L/2}$. Under RH this is a theorem
(\emph{sampling-floor}, Theorem~1: Beurling through the explicit formula).
Unconditionally the same quadratic form is Weil's pairing, not a Gram of
real samples: the contrapositive of \emph{global} positivity is RH, but
positivity on a single window $W_L$ does not force every zero onto the
line. An off-line zero produces a negative direction once the window is
large enough to see it; below that scale the form can stay positive.
The route does not prove RH. It says, in the language of Beurling density
and de~Branges spaces, where the positivity of $Q_L$ lives, and which
tools of that theory can enter.
\end{abstract}

\section{The sampling reading, already a theorem}
Fix $L>0$, $\tau=L/2$. Even $f\in L^2([-L/2,L/2])$ have Fourier transforms
$F\in PW_\tau$. Under RH the explicit formula on this window is
\[
Q_L(f)=\sum_{\gamma}F(\gamma)^2,
\]
so
\[
c_L=\inf_{f\ne0}\frac{Q_L(f)}{\|f\|^2}
\]
is $2\pi$ times the lower sampling constant of $\Gamma=\{\pm\gamma\}$ for
$PW_\tau$. Beurling--Landau: a separated subset $\Lambda\subset\Gamma$ of
lower uniform density $D^-(\Lambda)>\tau/\pi$ is a sampling set. The zeros
have $N(T+1)-N(T)\to\infty$, so such a $\Lambda$ exists for every $\tau$.
Hence $c_L>0$ and $\lambda_{\min}(V_N)\downarrow c_L$
(\emph{sampling-floor}, Theorem~1). Nothing further is claimed here.

The size of $c_L$ is not a density statement. Density only gives $c_L>0$.
The desert $(-\gamma_1,\gamma_1)$ and the gaps longer than the Nyquist
step $2\pi/L$ fix the \emph{exponential} smallness
(\emph{sampling-floor} Prop.~2, geometric law of
\emph{depth-geometry-quorum-mechanism}). Beurling's $D^->$ is too coarse
to see $e^{-L\gamma_1}$.

\section{Unconditional identity: Weil, not a real Gram}
Without RH the left side $Q_L$ is still the prime-side pairing (pole $+$
archimedean $-$ towers $n\le e^L$). The right side is
\[
Q_L(f)=\sum_{\rho}\hat g_f(\rho),
\]
sum over \emph{all} nontrivial zeros $\rho$, with $\hat g_f$ the entire
function attached to $f$ by the explicit formula (Weil--Bombieri
normalization of the repository, App.~B of \emph{quorum-theorem}).
On the line, $\rho=\tfrac12+i\gamma$ and $\hat g_f(\rho)=F(\gamma)^2$
(even convention of the window). Off the line,
$\rho=\tfrac12+\sigma+i\gamma$ with $\sigma\ne0$, the term is not a
square of a real sample. The form is no longer a Gram of evaluators on
$\mathbb{R}$.

\begin{remark}
Global Weil positivity --- $Q(h)\ge0$ for every admissible $h$ on
$\mathbb{R}$, not just on one $W_L$ --- is equivalent to RH. That is
Weil's theorem. Restricting the test class to $W_L$ loses the
equivalence: a form that is positive on every compact window is a
stronger statement than positivity on one window, and is again RH.
\end{remark}

\section{Visibility of an off-line zero}
Let $\rho=\tfrac12+\sigma+i\gamma$ with $\sigma\ne0$. The contribution of
the pair $(\rho,1-\rho)$ to $Q_L$ is $2\mathrm{Re}\,\hat g_f(\rho)$.
A test function whose spectral mass sits at $\rho$ can make this term
dominate and negative.

\begin{lemma}[visibility, schematic]
If $F\in PW_\tau$ can be chosen with $|F(\sigma+i\gamma)|\ge c\|F\|$ and
$\sum_{t\in\mathbb{R}}|F(t)|^2$ small compared with that, then $Q_L$
takes a negative value as soon as the off-line term is included.
Such an $F$ exists in $PW_\tau$ once $\tau$ exceeds a constant times
$|\sigma|+1$ and the distance from $\gamma$ to the real sampling nodes
is not large compared with $\tau^{-1}$.
\end{lemma}
This is Paley--Wiener, not arithmetic: an entire function of type $\tau$
cannot peak far off the real axis unless $\tau$ is large enough
(Phragm\'en--Lindel\"of / Bernstein). Consequence for the repository:
positivity of $Q_L$ at $\mu=e^L=11$ is compatible with an off-line zero
at height $10^6$. It is not compatible with an off-line zero inside the
band of $V_N$ once $N$ resolves that height. A single certified window
does not force RH; a uniform statement over $L\to\infty$ would.

\paragraph{Wrong continuation.}
Continuing the Dirichlet-kernel formula $2\sin(sL/2)/(s\sqrt L)$ to
$s=\sigma+i\gamma$ explodes as $\sinh(\gamma L/2)$ and produces
$\lambda_{\min}\sim-10^7$ already at $\sigma=0.05$. That is the Fourier
transform in a complex \emph{frequency}, not the Weil pairing. Discarded.

\paragraph{Weil weight.}
The pairing at $\rho=\tfrac12+\sigma+i\gamma$ is
$\hat\eta(\rho)=\int_{-L/2}^{L/2}\eta(x)\,e^{\sigma x}e^{i\gamma x}\,dx$,
bounded by $e^{|\sigma|\tau}\sqrt L$. At $\sigma=0$ it recovers the
on-line formula. The quadratic term attached to the pair
$(\rho,1-\rho)$ is the Gram of
$(\hat\eta_n(\rho)\hat\eta_m(1-\rho))$. On $V_9$ at $\mu=11$, forty
real zeros already in $Q_{\mathrm{line}}$:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lcc}\toprule
$\sigma$ at $\gamma_1$ & $\lambda_{\min}$ of $Q_{\mathrm{line}}+F(\rho)F(1-\rho)$
  & of $Q_{\mathrm{line}}+|F(\rho)|^2$\\
\midrule
$0$ & $+3\times10^{-22}$ & $+3\times10^{-22}$\\
$0.01$ & $-5.4\times10^{-5}$ & $+3\times10^{-17}$\\
$0.10$ & $-7.3\times10^{-3}$ & $+4\times10^{-17}$\\
$0.50$ & $-0.23$ & $+4\times10^{-17}$\\
$1.00$ & $-1.28$ & $+3\times10^{-17}$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
$|F(\rho)|^2$ never crosses. $F(\rho)F(1-\rho)$ crosses at once, at
height $\gamma_1$, with size $O(\sigma^2)$ at small $\sigma$ --- not
$10^7$. The same extra term at height $80$ or $200$ leaves
$\lambda_{\min}$ at $10^{-22}$: this window does not see a high
off-line zero. Visibility is local in height, mild in $\sigma$, and
tied to $\gamma_1$. A certified $Q_{11}\succ0$ still allows an off-line
zero at large height. It does not allow one at $\gamma_1$ once the
pairing is identified with App.~B.

\paragraph{App.~B closes the ``if''.}
\emph{quorum-theorem}, Remark~(i) and App.~B: the implemented form is
Weil's pairing for $g=f\star\tilde f$, with $\hat g=|\hat f|^2$ on the
critical line, and
\[
Q(f)=\sum_\rho\hat g(\rho)
\]
unconditionally. On the line $\hat g(\tfrac12+i\gamma)=|\hat f(\gamma)|^2$.
Off the line $\hat g(\rho)=\hat f(\rho)\,\hat f(1-\rho)$. That is the
term measured above. The identification is the one already derived
for the repository, not a new convention. The factor $2$ in
$Q=\sum 2\,\hat c\hat c^\top$ is the pair $\pm\gamma$.

\section{What de~Branges supplies, and what it does not}
A de~Branges space $\mathcal{H}(E)$ is a Hilbert space of entire
functions with a reproducing kernel built from a Hermite--Biehler
function $E$. Paley--Wiener $PW_\tau$ is the model case
$E(z)=e^{-i\tau z}$. Sampling and interpolation in $\mathcal{H}(E)$ are
completely described (de~Branges, Beurling--Malliavin for the
PW case): a set is sampling when its density exceeds the type, with a
structure condition on the gaps.

Two precise contacts with $Q_L$.

\paragraph{PW as $\mathcal{H}(E)$.}
Theorem~1 is the PW-special case of ``the zeros sample $\mathcal{H}(E)$''.
The desert bound is the reproducing-kernel mass of the hole
$(-\gamma_1,\gamma_1)$ in that space --- Slepian is the first
eigenfunction of the projection onto the hole. Sub-Nyquist gaps are
the same statement for a union of holes. The geometric law is a
conjecture about the bottom eigenvalue of a Gram of reproducing
kernels at the zeros. de~Branges gives the language; it does not
give $a=1.69$, $b=0.82$.

\paragraph{The de~Branges approach to $\xi$.}
There is a different $E$, built from $\xi$, whose space $\mathcal{H}(E)$
has the zeros of $\xi$ as a distinguished set. RH is equivalent to
axioms on that $E$ (de~Branges 1986 and later variants). That $E$ is
\emph{not} $e^{-i\tau z}$. Importing a theorem about $PW_\tau$ into the
$\xi$-space is the usual gap in the programme. The windowed form
$Q_L$ lives in PW. The $\xi$-space lives on the critical strip. They
share a philosophy (positivity $\Leftrightarrow$ sampling on a line)
and not a kernel.

\section{What this route can still do in the repository}
\begin{enumerate}
\item State Theorem~1 as the definition of the floor, not as a
  numerical finding. Already done in \emph{sampling-floor}.
\item Replace ``hyper-nullity'' language by ``the sampling constant
  of a set with a hole of width $\gamma_1$ and type $\tau$''. Same
  numbers, right theory.
\item Prove a quantitative visibility lemma: an explicit $L(\sigma,\gamma)$
  beyond which an off-line pair at $\tfrac12+\sigma+i\gamma$ forces
  $\lambda_{\min}(Q_L)<0$. This is analysis, not flint.
\item Done on $V_9$ at $\mu=11$: the pair at $\gamma_1$ crosses;
  height $80$ does not. A quantitative $L(\sigma,\gamma)$ for every
  height remains analysis.
\item Do \emph{not} claim that $c_L>0$ for all $L$ unconditionally.
  That statement is RH.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Status}
Theorem~1 (under RH) is already in the living corpus. The present note
adds the unconditional reading, the Weil-weight visibility measurement,
the identification $\hat g(\rho)=\hat f(\rho)\hat f(1-\rho)$ with App.~B,
and the boundary between PW-de~Branges and $\xi$-de~Branges. No new
theorem. No movement of RH. The branch can stop here.
