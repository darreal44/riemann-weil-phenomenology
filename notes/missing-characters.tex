\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The depth map was trained on fourteen of the seventeen real primitive
characters of conductor $\le24$. The three held out --- $\chi_{-8}$,
$\chi_{-20}$, $\chi_{-23}$ --- are now instantiated: Kronecker tables,
completed $\Lambda$-functions, first zeros harvested, and drilling
speeds predicted from the four-variable map. Measured $s$ is not
computed in this commit; the predictions are preregistered.
\end{abstract}

\section{Why these three}

Real primitive characters are Kronecker characters $\chi_d$ of
fundamental discriminants. Conductor $\le24$ gives seventeen of them.
The working family kept fourteen and dropped the three odd characters
$\chi_{-8}$, $\chi_{-20}$, $\chi_{-23}$ (the even $\chi_{+8}$ is
already in the map). $\chi_{-23}$ was flagged as the best next test:
class number $3$, small gap, low conductor mass.

\section{Preregistered speeds}

The map of~\cite{Depth},
$\hat s\approx0.14\,\gamma_1^{1.32}(\gamma_2-\gamma_1)^{0.45}
e^{-0.13 D}\,1.32^{[\mathrm{odd}]}$, gives
\[
\hat s(\chi_{-8})=1.47,\qquad
\hat s(\chi_{-20})=0.57,\qquad
\hat s(\chi_{-23})=0.76.
\]
A measured slope off its prediction by more than $20\%$ on any of the
three is a kill of the map as written. $\chi_{-8}$ is the internal
control: its even twin $\chi_{+8}$ already sits at $s=1.47\pm0.05$,
and the map sends the odd twin to the same number --- parity and the
larger gap of $\chi_{-8}$ cancel. $\chi_{-20}$ is a high-$D$ small-$\gamma_1$
point, the regime where $\chi_{12}$ already pulls $+14\%$.
$\chi_{-23}$ is the genuine out-of-sample: small gap, low $D$, class
number $3$.

\section{How to measure $s$}

The three characters are registered in $\mathtt{code/dscan.py}$ as
\texttt{chim8}, \texttt{chi20}, \texttt{chi23}. Replay:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 dscan.py chim8
python3 dscan.py chi20
python3 dscan.py chi23
\end{verbatim}
Each call runs $\mu=5.5$ ($N=25$) then $\mu=11$ ($N=41$). Two windows
give a two-point slope; a third window ($\mu=16$ or $22$) is needed
before the slope is compared to $\hat s$ at the advertised
$\pm0.05$--$0.1$ budget. Nothing in this commit claims that
measurement.


\section{Measured slopes}

Engine: \texttt{code/scan\_s.py}, validated on $\chi_3$ at $\mu=11$
($\lambda_{\min}=5\cdot10^{-16}$, $\ell_0=35.2$). Last-segment slopes:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule
$\chi$ & segment & $s_{\mathrm{meas}}$ & $\hat s$ & err \\
\midrule
$\chi_{-8}$  & $16\to22$ & $1.46$ & $1.47$ & $-0.5\%$ \\
$\chi_{-20}$ & $22\to32$ & $0.55$ & $0.57$ & $-3.7\%$ \\
$\chi_{-23}$ & $22\to32$ & $0.47$ & $0.76$ & $-38\%$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
$\chi_{-8}$ and $\chi_{-20}$ confirm the map. $\chi_{-23}$ does not:
the slope stabilized ($0.47$ on both $16\to22$ and $22\to32$). The map
over-weights the gap $1.34$. A larger basis can only raise $s$
(artifact 7); it will not close a $38\%$ hole by itself.

\section{Status}

Zeros and predictions are in. Slopes are in. Two confirmations, one kill ($\chi_{-23}$).
