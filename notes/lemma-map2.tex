\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The published four-variable map is dead on $\chi_{-23}$ ($-29\%$).
The candidate that remains after dropping the gap and $D$ is
$s=c\,\gamma_1^\alpha\,\rho^{[\mathrm{odd}]}$. Fitted on the thirteen
stationary rows of the depth-note table it predicts $\hat s(\chi_{-23})=1.06$
against $0.54$ measured: $+97\%$. The two-variable map dies harder
than the four-variable one. The guilty axis is parity: $\chi_{-23}$ is
odd and drills like even. A refit $\gamma_1+\mathrm{gap}+D$
\emph{without} parity lands at $0.45$ against $0.54$ ($-16\%$). That
is not a new map --- it is the diagnosis of the kill.
\end{abstract}

\section{The candidate}

After notebook 18 the four-variable law
\[
\hat s \approx 0.14\,\gamma_1^{1.32}(\gamma_2-\gamma_1)^{0.45}
e^{-0.13 D}\,1.32^{[\mathrm{odd}]}
\]
is dead on its documented fragility axes. The leftover proposal was
S2 stripped to two variables: desert and parity, the two inversions
already in the depth note ($\chi_4>\chi_5$, $\chi_7>\chi_8$).

Engine \texttt{code/map2.py}, training set = the thirteen stationary
rows of~\cite{Depth} (uniformized at $\mu=30$--$38$; $\chi_{15}$
dropped as rising; $\zeta$ kept out as a pole). Least squares in
$\log s$:
\[
s \;=\; 0.171\,\gamma_1^{1.327}\,1.54^{[\mathrm{odd}]}.
\]
Training rms $21\%$, against $6\%$ for the four-variable law.

\section{Parity is the axis}

$\chi_{-23}$ is odd, $\gamma_1=2.87$. Even characters at that desert:
$\chi_{24}^{\mathrm{e}}$ ($\gamma_1=2.69$, $s=0.50$), $\chi_{21}$
($2.32$, $0.58$). Odd characters at that desert: $\chi_{11}$
($2.48$, $1.07$). Measured $s(\chi_{-23})=0.54$ sits on the
\emph{even} locus. The factor $1.54^{[\mathrm{odd}]}$ is what
inflates $1.06$.

A three-variable refit with no parity,
$\log s = a + b\log\gamma_1 + c\log(\mathrm{gap}) + d\log D$,
gives $\hat s(\chi_{-23})=0.45$ against $0.54$ ($-16\%$, inside the
old $20\%$ kill). Training rms $17\%$. This is a diagnosis, not a
successor map: it was read on the same hold-out that killed the
predecessor. The next map, if there is one, has to be
preregistered before the next character.

\section{Status}

$s=s(\gamma_1,\mathrm{parity})$ is false. S2 remains: $s$ is
$-d\log|\hat v_0(\gamma_1)|/d\mu$. The arithmetic of that Lyapunov
exponent is not parity-times-desert. $\chi_{-23}$ is the witness.
