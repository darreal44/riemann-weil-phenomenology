\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The certified quorum theorem is a statement at three fixed windows
($340$ witnesses). What remains is the statement at every scale: every
proper sub-Euler product of the windowed Weil form is indefinite, for
every $\mu$. The reduction already measured at $\mu=11$ cuts this to
two harmonic-analysis lemmas on the radical --- (B1)~diagonal silence
before recruitment, (B2)~a surviving off-diagonal coupling --- plus
the observation that the radical dimension grows like the ladder.
Neither lemma mentions RH. We state both, record what is certified,
and isolate the one estimate that still scales.
\end{abstract}

\section{What is already a theorem}

On the $47$-dimensional even window $V$ at $\mu=11$, and on the
analogous windows at $\mu=16$ and $\mu=22$ (and for $\chi_3$ at
$\mu=11$), every proper partial form $Q_S$ admits an explicit negative
witness, ball-certified~\cite{Quorum}. That is $340$ cases, no
exception. It is a theorem about explicitly defined matrices. It is
not a theorem about every $\mu$.

At $\mu=11$ a further reduction is measured exact~\cite{LabNB} \S15:
the violator of $Q_{\setminus p}$ lives $99.4\%$ in the quasi-radical
of the complete $Q$, and
\[
\lambda_{\min}(Q_{\setminus p})
\;=\;
\lambda_{\min}(M_p)+O(10^{-4}),
\]
where $M_p$ is the tower of $p$ restricted to the radical. Each $M_p$
has a certified negative $2\times2$ minor (margins $0.10$--$0.19$
against quadrature $<10^{-8}$). The pattern of those minors is the
content:
a \emph{silent} rung (diagonal $\approx0$: $p$ not yet recruited)
paired with a \emph{speaking} rung, off-diagonal of size $\sim0.4$.
A matrix $\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}\varepsilon&b\\b&a\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$
is indefinite as soon as $b^2>\varepsilon a$, which is automatic when
$\varepsilon\approx0$.

\section{The two lemmas}

Let $v_0,v_1,\dots$ be the quasi-null eigenfunctions of the complete
form $Q$ at a window $\mu=e^L$, and write
$k(p)$ for the first rung that recruits $p$ (so $k(p)=n-1$ when $p$
is the $n$-th $\chi$-supported prime, empirically). Write
$(f\star g)(x)=\Theta_{f,g}(x)$ for the symmetrized correlation.

\begin{lemma}[B1, diagonal silence]\label{lem:B1}
For every interior prime $p$ and every rung $j<k(p)$,
\[
(v_j\star v_j)(\log p)\;=\;o_p(1).
\]
Equivalently the diagonal entry of $M_p$ on a pre-recruitment rung
vanishes.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[B2, surviving coupling]\label{lem:B2}
For every interior prime $p$ there is at least one speaking rung $k$
such that
\[
(v_j\star v_k)(\log p)\;\ne\;0
\qquad\text{for some silent }j<k(p).
\]
The coupling is $O(1)$, not $o(1)$: it does not shrink with $\mu$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{conjecture}[Quorum at every scale]
B1 $+$ B2 $+$ the radical reduction $\Rightarrow$ $Q_S$ is indefinite
for every proper $S\subsetneq P(\mu)$ and every $\mu$ large enough
that $V$ is depth-adequate. No appeal to RH.
\end{conjecture}

Lemma~A of~\cite{Quorum} (every single tower is indefinite on $V$) is
elementary and already proved; it does not locate the negative
directions. B1 and B2 do.

\section{What scales, what does not}

The $2\times2$ certificate is dimension-free: once a silent/speaking
pair exists, indefiniteness of $M_p$ does not care how large the
radical is. The radical dimension itself grows --- $\ell_0=s\mu$
produces $\sim s\mu/\Delta_\infty$ rungs --- so B1 and B2 have to be
checked against a growing flag, not a fixed $8\times8$ block.

The estimate that still scales is the reduction
$\lambda_{\min}(Q_{\setminus p})=\lambda_{\min}(M_p)+O(\varepsilon(\mu))$.
At $\mu=11$, $\varepsilon\sim10^{-4}$ against
$\lambda_{\min}(M_p)\sim-0.5$. As $\mu$ grows the complete form sits
deeper ($\lambda_{\min}(Q)\sim e^{-s\mu}$) and the bulk gap above the
ladder stays $O(1)$. A perturbation argument on that gap gives
$\varepsilon(\mu)\to0$, which is the friendly direction. Writing the
$\varepsilon(\mu)$ bound in ball arithmetic at one more window
($\mu=16$, radical already computed by the quorum engine) would
promote the reduction from ``measured exact at $11$'' to ``certified
in a range''.

B1 is the microscopic content of the recruitment law. A proof of B1
is a proof that $v_j\star v_j$ is supported, at leading order, on the
logs of the primes already recruited by rung $j$. That is a statement
about the correlation of windowed eigenfunctions at a discrete set of
lags --- harmonic analysis on the window, plus the seating of primes,
not RH.

B2 is the assertion that silence is \emph{diagonal only}. The
off-diagonal $(v_j\star v_k)(\log p)$ is an oscillatory overlap with
no symmetry that forces a zero. One non-vanishing pair per $p$
suffices.

\section{A finite check that would change the status}

At $\mu=16$ the quorum engine already built every $Q_S$. Restricting
the four simple-deletion violators to the quasi-radical of the
complete form and certifying one $2\times2$ minor per prime would
repeat the $\mu=11$ lemma-B campaign at a second scale. If the silent
/ speaking pattern recurs, B1+B2 stop being a $\mu=11$ anecdote.

\section{Status}

The quorum at three windows is a theorem. B1 and B2 are the lemmas
for every scale. The reduction $\varepsilon(\mu)$ is the one analytic
estimate left. None of the three mentions RH.

\section*{Addendum --- $\mu=16$ witnesses, 1 September}

The shipped file \texttt{witnesses\_zeta\_mu16.json} already certifies
every proper $Q_S$ negative at this window (theorem). Overlap of each
simple-deletion violator with the complete-form ground state alone is
$0.04$--$0.40$ --- not $0.99$ as at $\mu=11$ against the six-dimensional
radical. That is expected: the radical has grown. The $2\times2$ minor
campaign at $\mu=16$ still needs the complete eigenspace, which is not
an artifact. B1+B2 remain lemmas; the cheap lock that would promote
them is ``assemble $Q$ at $\mu=16$, project $T_p$ on the first eight
eigenvectors, certify one minor per interior prime.''

\section*{Addendum --- $\zeta$ at $\mu=16$, 1 September morning}

\texttt{code/lemma\_B\_mu16.py}. Window $\mu=16$, interior primes
$\{2,3,5,7,11,13\}$. At $N=37$, $R=8$: four of six primes have a
negative minor; $11$ and $13$ are silent. At $N=29$, $R=12$:

must grow with the window. B1+B2 hold for $\zeta$ at $\mu=16$ once
the radical slice includes the late speakers. Depth of $\lambda_0$ is
still basis-short of $N\sim71$; the minors are a statement about
indefiniteness of $T_p$ on a computed bottom space, not about the
razor.
