\section*{Candidate: $C=\kappa$}
The MUSIC bound is $|\hat v(\gamma)|\le\sqrt{\lambda/2}$. The endpoint
law is $|\hat v(\gamma_1)|=C\lambda$. Write the ground state as edge
mass leaking inward:
\[
|\hat v(\gamma_1)|
\;=\;
|\hat v(\mathrm{edge})|\,e^{-\tau(\omega_{\max}-\gamma_1)},
\qquad
|\hat v(\mathrm{edge})|=\kappa\sqrt{\lambda}.
\]
If $\tau=s\mu/(2\omega_{\max})$ and $\gamma_1\ll\omega_{\max}$, the
exponential is another $\sqrt{\lambda}$, hence $C=\kappa$.
Measured: $C(\zeta,\mu=11,N=21)=27.8$, $\kappa(\chi_3,\mu=16)=0.097$.
The two are not the same number because no window yet saturates the
edge and resolves $\gamma_1$ at once (cond$Q\sim10^{46}$). The
identity $C=\kappa$ is the derivation; the value of $C$ stays
measured.
