\begin{abstract}
\noindent
For the semi-local Weil quadratic form on the window $[-\log\sqrt\mu,\log\sqrt\mu]$ (Connes--Consani framework), we measure the smallest
eigenvalue $\lambda_{\min}(\mu)$ for $\zeta$ and fourteen of the seventeen real
primitive Dirichlet characters of conductor $q\le24$, with matched,
depth-adequate Galerkin bases up to $\mu=38$. Four structural facts
emerge. (i) The depth obeys an \emph{affine law}
$-\ln\lambda_{\min}=s\,\mu+b$ for the entire family, $\zeta$ included
($s_\zeta=11.7\pm0.2$; the previously apparent bend was a basis
artifact); linearity in $\mu$ is the signature of the integer lattice ---
any continuum mechanism caps at $O(\log\mu)$. (ii) The drilling speeds
$s$, spanning $0.46$ to $11.7$, are described at $\sim\!6\%$ by an
empirical four-variable map in $(\gamma_1,\gamma_2-\gamma_1,D,
\text{parity})$; the map survived two adversarial preregistered tests and
failed a third ($\chi_{17}$, small first gap $\gamma_2-\gamma_1$), so it is presented as a
map, not a law. Counting-type functionals are ruled out wholesale: the
zero-counting deficit of the desert is quasi-universal
($\approx0.5$ missing zero for every character), so \emph{depth is
a bandwidth phenomenon in absolute frequencies, not a counting one}.
(iii) The quasi-null ladders of all fifteen $L$-functions share one
architecture: rung spacings collapse onto a single spacing-vs-level
profile $\Delta(\ell)$ ($\pm12\%$, $\zeta$ indistinguishable from the
characters at equal level); a dilated-Slepian reading of this profile was
proposed and then dissolved by its own precision campaign. Radical
spectroscopy reveals the microscopic organization: \emph{rung $k$
recruits the $(k{+}1)$-th $\chi$-supported prime}, in order, with tower
signs modulated by $\chi(p)$. (iv) Positivity itself is a \emph{quorum}
of the Euler product: every proper sub-product violates it by $O(1)$
while the complete product lands on the razor's edge ($\zeta$ at
$\mu=11$: $3.58\times10^{-48}$ in our basis) --- certified
computationally at fixed windows ($\mu=11,16,22$, and $\chi_3$ at
$\mu=11$) in the companion paper, which also reduces the all-scales
statement to two harmonic-analysis assertions. All claims here are
measurements with stated error bars; every code path, zero cache and
artifact is public.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

For $\mu=e^L$ and a real primitive character $\chi$ mod $q$ (or $\zeta$,
$q=1$, with its pole term), the semi-local Weil form $Q_\chi$ acts on
even test functions supported in $[-L/2,L/2]$: archimedean term in a
closed Frullani representation on $[0,L]$ (so the finite-$T$ archimedean
artifact of the literature is structurally absent), minus the
$\chi$-signed prime sum over $p^k\le\mu$, plus, for $\zeta$, the pole
term. The family was assembled incrementally during the campaign; the three
odd characters of discriminant $-8,-20,-23$ were not computed and are
the natural next out-of-sample tests (\S6). Matrices are assembled in
the shifted-cosine basis with the exact
correlation table (derived and certified in the companion paper
\cite{Quorum}, App.~A--B: the form is \emph{exactly} Weil's pairing,
$Q(f)=\sum_\gamma|\hat f(\gamma)|^2$, no normalization factor), and
diagonalized in multiprecision (45--110 digits). Every matrix is
validated against the zero side of the explicit formula (40--280 zeros
per $L$-function, computed independently).

Two systematic effects are
controlled throughout, both documented in the lab notebook
\cite{LabNB}: \emph{transients} (narrow-desert characters approach their
asymptotic slope slowly, from below, so slopes at small cutoffs are
lower bounds --- corrections up to $+18\%$), and \emph{basis depth
demand} (an under-sized basis inflates $\lambda_{\min}$ by orders of
magnitude and fakes a downward bend of deep ladders; $\zeta$'s apparent
non-linearity was exactly this artifact, and its ladder is linear,
$s_\zeta=11.7\pm0.2$, intercept $\approx-20$ --- reconciling the
Connes--Consani $\sim\!10\mu$ heuristic, which is this affine law read at
$\mu=11$).

The empirical description. \emph{Protocol}: the training set is the
twelve characters of Table~1 minus the two final preregistered tests
($\chi_{21}$, $\chi_{17}$); $\chi_{19}$ was itself a preregistered
test of an earlier round, then absorbed; $\chi_{15}$ enters with its
transient-corrected estimate; $\zeta$ is outside the map (pole;
anomaly ratio $\rho\approx1.3$ against the even baseline). On these
twelve points,
\[
\ln s \;=\; 1.32\,\ln\gamma_1 \;+\; 0.45\,\ln(\gamma_2-\gamma_1)
\;-\;0.13\,D\;+\;0.28\,[\text{odd}]\;-\;1.95,
\]
RMS $6.1\%$, leave-one-out median $4.8\%$. Its stress record, all
preregistered: $\gamma_1$ alone and ``prime conductors stay high'' were
\emph{killed} by $\chi_{19}$ ($\gamma_1=1.516$, the narrowest desert,
predicted $0.28$ resp.\ $\approx0.9$, measured $0.58$); the twin pair
mod $24$ (equal removed primes, opposite parities) established the
arithmetic-density variable against $\gamma_1$ (decisive pair
$\chi_{11}$ vs $\chi_{24}^{\mathrm e}$: nearly equal $\gamma_1$, slopes
$1.07$ vs $0.50$) --- and the anti-transient campaign designed to test
this contrast \emph{widened} it; $\chi_{21}$ landed at $+9\%$ of its
preregistered prediction; $\chi_{17}$ missed by $-26\%$, exactly where
the gap variable extrapolates below its fitted range
($\gamma_2-\gamma_1=1.91$), and the refit on fourteen points moves the
exponents ($0.45\to0.68$) --- parameter instability. Honest status:
\emph{a map accurate to $\sim\!10\%$ with charted failure modes, not a
closed law}.

\section{Bandwidth, not counting}

Every counting-type candidate for the ``single variable'' died, and the
autopsy is the finding. The desert normalized by local zero spacing
fails the decisive pair; the Laplace count $L(\tau)=\sum e^{-\gamma_k/\tau}$
conflates density with landscape ($\zeta$ predicted at $39.6$ against
$11.7$); and the \emph{vacancy} $V(\tau)=\int(\bar N_\chi-N_\chi)
e^{-t/\tau}dt$ --- built from the exact $\Gamma$-phase smooth counting,
whose calibration reproduced $c\approx0$ for all fourteen characters and
$c=1.006$ for $\zeta$, the classical $+1$ --- turns out to be
\emph{quasi-universal}: $V\in[-0.10,+0.05]$ across a family whose $s$
spans a factor $25$. In counting units every desert holds the same
$\approx\tfrac12$ missing zero, because the first zero sits where the
smooth count reaches $\tfrac12$; there is nothing to read. The only
non-trivial vacancy in the family is $\zeta$'s ($V=+0.401$: the pole's
push, visible at last in a counting observable, yet useless to the
model). Meanwhile the variables that work ($\gamma_1$,
$\gamma_2-\gamma_1$) are \emph{absolute frequencies}: the ladder measures
the room, in hertz, where a test function can hide its spectrum --- a
Slepian time-bandwidth phenomenon, not a statistics of zeros.

\section{One ladder, drilled at fifteen speeds}

\paragraph{Linearity is the lattice.} Since the depth grows like
$s\mu$, the effective concentration budget grows \emph{linearly} in
$\mu$; but any mechanism carried by the continuum window --- prolates
against a fixed band --- caps at $O(\gamma L)=O(\log\mu)$, and so does
the Landau count of the desert over the multiplicative lattice. Linear
depth requires each \emph{integer} of the window to contribute a bounded
increment: the drilling is a discrete-lattice phenomenon (the
Connes--Consani map $E$ is its embodiment), invisible to continuum
analysis alone. A zeroth-order lattice estimate,
$c_{\mathrm{eff}}\approx\gamma_1 N_\chi(\mu)/2\pi$, holds to a factor
$1.9\pm0.9$ across the factor-$25$ family (an order-of-magnitude
estimate, not a fit) --- right scale, wrong details; band and lattice do not factorize (the recurring $\chi_{17}$
anomaly marks the coupling).

\paragraph{The universal rung profile.} Across $33$ rungs of the fifteen
quasi-null ladders, the spacing of a rung (in $-\ln\lambda$ units)
depends only on its depth level $\ell$, not on the character: by level
bands, $10.9\pm1.5$ on $[0,15)$, $13.5\pm1.5$ on $[15,40)$,
$16.0\pm1.6$ on $[40,80)$, $16.0\pm2.1$ on $[80,150)$ --- $\zeta$'s
rungs interleave indistinguishably with $\chi_3$'s and $\chi_4$'s at
equal level; fitted profile $\Delta(\ell)\approx9.8+0.65\sqrt\ell$ (RMS
$1.8$). A ``dilated Slepian'' reading, $\lambda_k\approx
(1-\lambda_k^{\mathrm{Slepian}})^\kappa$ with $\kappa=2.85\pm0.26$, was
proposed and then \emph{dissolved by its own precision campaign}:
ten-rung ladders fit the Fuchs form beautifully per ladder, but the
fitted exponent does not transfer ($3.5$--$5.4$ with $c$ free) --- the
model has no well-defined $\kappa$, and ``$e$ or $3$?'' was ill-posed.
What survives, model-free, is the $\Delta(\ell)$ collapse itself: the
problem factorizes into a universal architecture (a harmonic-analysis
target, blind to arithmetic) times one number per $L$-function, the
drilling speed $s(\chi)$.

\paragraph{The recruitment law.} Radical spectroscopy --- projecting each
component form (pole, archimedean, one tower per prime) onto the bottom
eigenstates --- reveals the organization: \emph{rung $k$ recruits the
$(k{+}1)$-th $\chi$-supported prime, in order}, the next prime whispering
at the $10^{-2}$ level one rung before its turn. For $\zeta$ the ground
state is a pole/archimedean interferometer ($+1.502/-1.443$) trimmed by
the $2$-tower alone ($-0.058$), all other primes dead to three decimals;
for $\chi_3$ the same staircase runs $2,5,7,11,\dots$ ($3$ skipped,
$\chi_3(3)=0$) with tower signs modulated by $\chi(p)$ --- where
$\chi_3(2)=-1$, the $2$-tower \emph{supports} the ground state
($+0.345$ against an archimedean $-0.347$): $\zeta$'s roles reversed.
Since each new prime power $p^k\le\mu$ reinforces the rungs that
recruited $p$, and the $\Lambda$-weighted count of prime powers is
$\psi(\mu)\approx\mu$, the linearity of the drilling is the prime number
theorem seen from the radical.

\section{Positivity is a quorum}

Recruitment describes the \emph{organization} of the completed form's
ladder; its \emph{existence} is a different and sharper fact. Every
partial Euler product inside the window has a negative smallest
eigenvalue of order $1$ --- at $\mu=11$: $\lambda_{\min}$ from $-0.52$ to
$-1.15$ over all $2^4-1$ proper subsets of $\{2,3,5,7\}$, the residual
negatives being the radical-side face of the off-line pseudo-zeros of
truncated Euler products --- and the moment the last interior prime
joins, the form lands on the complete $48$-digit-deep ladder: a
razor's-edge landing at every scale, with depth \emph{not decomposable}
into per-prime contributions. This is now a certified theorem at fixed windows
(\cite{Quorum}: $340$ proper sub-products violated, exhaustively at
$\mu=11,16,22$ and for $\chi_3$ without pole, ball-arithmetic witnesses
frozen in the repository; the all-scales quorum remains a conjecture), whose mechanism --- a prime cannot be
diagonally silent on a rung while coupling to it from another without
creating a negative direction --- reduces the general quorum to two
harmonic-analysis assertions (diagonal silence before recruitment;
survival of the coupling), neither mentioning RH. One consequence
deserves emphasis: since the complete form sits at $e^{-s\mu}$ of zero,
\emph{no inequality with slack can prove Weil positivity at these
scales}; only an identity --- exhibiting the truncated form as the sum
of squares it secretly is --- can. Measured, this is the sharpest
explanation we know of why generic analytic approaches to RH fail: the
margin they would need does not exist.

\section{Open problems}

Derive the universal profile $\Delta(\ell)$ (the surviving lemma
candidate; pure harmonic analysis). Derive $s(\chi)$ (the coupling of
window arithmetic to the drill; zeroth order $\gamma_1N_\chi/2\pi$,
structure open). Prove the quorum at every scale via the
silence/coupling reduction. And, for the seismograph program: with a
$\sim\!10\%$ map, the anomaly statistic $\rho(\chi)=
s_{\mathrm{meas}}/s_{\mathrm{map}}$ is calibrated ($\zeta$, the one
member with a pole, registers $\rho\approx1.3$ against the even
baseline); scanning real even characters at growing conductor is
mechanical, the cost growing with the kernel width
$\tfrac12\log(3q/\pi)$. (A Siegel zero is a \emph{real} zero, not a
small $\gamma_1$: the statistic $\rho$, calibrated on the critical-line
desert, would register an exceptional modulus through the map's failure,
not as a narrow desert per se.) The three uncomputed odd characters
($d=-8,-20,-23$) are the cheapest next tests; $\chi_{-23}$, odd with
small $\gamma_1$ at nearly the cost of $\chi_{19}$, is worth more than
any refinement of exponents.
