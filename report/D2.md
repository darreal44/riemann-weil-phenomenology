# The weight D₂(y)

    D₂(y) = 2 e^{−2 s₀ y} / (1 − e^{−2y})
          = 2 ∑_{k≥0} e^{−2 (s₀+k) y}

s₀ is the shift of the
Gamma factor on the
critical line:
GL1 even a=0 → s₀=1/4,
GL1 odd  a=1 → s₀=3/4,
GL2      two panels s₀=1/2 and 1.

## Where it comes from

The archimedean side of
the explicit formula is
a digamma of the
completed Gamma
(Γ_ℝ(s)=π^{−s/2}Γ(s/2),
or Γ_ℂ). After writing
ψ(z) = −γ + ∑_{k≥0}
(1/(k+1) − 1/(k+z))
and pairing with the
test function, the
Mellin / Laplace
variable y (dual to the
imaginary part) produces
a geometric series in
e^{−2y}: the poles of
Gamma sit at 0,−2,−4,…
shifted by 2s₀.

The factor 2 in front is
the even unfolding
(y and the copy at the
functional equation).
It matches the 2 in
θ₀₀(0)=2.

## What it does in A

    A_{nm} ⊃ ½ ∫_0^L D₂(y)
              ( F₀ e^{−(2−2s₀)y}
                − θ_{nm}(y) ) dy

D₂ is positive and
decreases. For s₀=1/4,
D₂ ∼ e^{−y/2} at large y:
that is why A₀₀ saturates
(`A00-L.md`) instead of
growing like L.

Near y=0, 1−e^{−2y} ∼ 2y,
so D₂ ∼ e^{−2s₀ y} / y.
The 1/y is cancelled in
the integrand by
g(0)=F₀−θ(0)=0 on the
diagonal (the pair
F₀ EC − θ vanishes at
0). That cancellation is
why the integral is
ordinary, not principal
value.

## What D₂ is not

Not a friction kernel
(`friction-not.md`).
Not the Slepian weight.
Not a measure on the
zeros. It is the
archimedean place,
written as a function
of the same y that
indexes θ(log n).
