# Semantics of the labels

Each word is a
predicate on
(p, μ, Q), not a
property of p.

    mute         λ₀(Q_{−p}) = λ₀(Q)
                 or p>μ
    dispensable  λ₀(Q_{−p}) > 0
    necessary    λ₀(Q_{−p}) < 0
    edge         log p / log μ ≥ 0.95
    interior     log p / log μ < 0.95
    heavy        |a_p| / 2√p ≥ 0.8
    delayed      ∃ μ₀>p with
                 dispensable(p,μ₀)
                 and necessary(p,μ)
                 for some μ>μ₀

“Necessary” does
not mean “the
form is false
without RH”. It
means this
cutoff’s matrix
goes indefinite
if that term is
omitted. Full Q
can stay PSD
(37a1 does).

“Delayed” is
existential over
μ, not a clock.
83 is delayed
because 84 is a
witness of
dispensable and
100 of
necessary.
3 at 62–84 then
86 is delayed
in the same
formal sense
(the plateau
was the
dispensable
stay). We
reserved the
word for 83
because the
stay sat *on
arrival*. That
restriction is
narrative, not
in the
predicate.

Heavy and edge
are
dimensionless
ratios. They do
not imply a
vote
(89 is edge and
dispensable;
3 at 84 is
interior and
dispensable;
83 at 100 is
edge and
necessary).
