# Taxonomy of μ

μ is not a dial on
one form. It is the
cutoff of the
explicit sum and
the length L=log μ
of the window.
Different μ are
different operators
P_μ, hence different
quorums.

## Kinds of window

**Desert.** μ before
the first prime that
the form needs.
Q is A (or A−T₂
only). χ₄ at μ=3:
A already SPD.
χ₅ at μ=3: A
indefinite, T₂
cancels.

**Arrival.** μ=p⁺.
θ(log p)=0. The new
term is mute as a
quadratic form on
the old ground
state, but it can
already move λ₀ of
a *drop* (83:
+0.087→+0.048).
Mute ≠ invisible.

**Interior.** y/L
has left 1. θ
grows toward 2I.
A prime can become
necessary here
without a new
arrival (3 on
(84,100]).

**Plateau.** A drop
sits at a small
positive number
across several μ
(3 at 62–84,
+0.09). Not a
floor. It died
the next time a
heavy prime had
time to couple.

**Cliff.** Sign
change of a drop
in a short
interval. Here:
(84,100], two
primes at once
(3 and 83).

**Deep well.** Full
λ₀ ~ 10^{-n} with
n rising, ℓ
rising. 37a1 at
250: ℓ=44, still
PSD. Depth ≠
quorum completed
for every drop.

## Labels on a prime,
indexed by μ

    mute          not in the sum, or
                  drop-p = full
    dispensable   drop-p >0
    necessary     drop-p <0
    edge          y/L ≳ 0.95
    heavy         |a_p|/2√p ≳ 0.8

83 at 84: edge,
heavy, dispensable.
83 at 100: edge,
heavy, necessary.
Same labels except
the vote.

## What μ does not
classify

c_L^* on W_L
(L=log 3 is one
μ=3, the class
is the whole
PW space).
Fmat Λ is another
cutoff; do not
add it to this
list.
