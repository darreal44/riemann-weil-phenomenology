# The Pólya “constant”

Two meanings, neither yet a C that flips χ₃.

## 1. A number in front of 1/|n−m|

|θ_nm|≤2/(π k), k=|n−m|. ∫_δ^L D₂ is finite (s₀=3/4, δ=0.05: 2.57). Then
|½ ∫_δ^L D₂ θ| ≤ 2.57/(π k) ≈0.82/k. ∑_k 0.82/k diverges. An absolute
Pólya constant in front of 1/k cannot give a finite row sum. Gershgorin
on |Q_nm| stays blocked at the harmonic series.

The true ∫ D₂ θ is smaller (oscillation). The constant one needs is a
decay better than 1/k after integration: Riemann–Lebesgue or an explicit
antiderivative of D₂ sin(ω_n y). That is not π and not 2.57.

## 2. Pólya frequency / convex tail

Already tried as a PD extension and rejected as a proof. No new constant
there.

Measured ∫_δ^L D₂ and ∑ 1/(k(2n+k)) at n=32 (0.074) live in the repo
now. They do not assemble into S_lo>0 without the oscillatory gain.

Not RH.
