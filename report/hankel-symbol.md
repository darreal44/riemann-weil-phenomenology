# Hankel symbol

The Hankel matrix [c_{n+m}] has symbol φ(e^{iθ}) = ∑_{k≥1} c_k e^{-ikθ}
(negative Fourier part). For c_k=1/k, φ = −log(1−e^{-iθ}) up to a
constant. |φ| is unbounded near θ=0 (the log singularity) and the
distance to H^∞ is π in the Hilbert normalisation.

The true Q off-diagonal is not this {c_k}. Its mean on each lag follows
½/(n+m) (Hankel) plus an oscillating Toeplitz-like term. There is no
single φ on the circle whose Hankel is Off_Q.

Writing φ_Q is not a step. Nehari needs a Hankel symbol; Off_Q does not
have one.

Not RH.
