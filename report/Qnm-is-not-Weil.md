# Is Q_nm Weil’s Q?

No. Same theme as Two Grams: one form, two matrices, neither is the full
explicit formula until every term is there.

**What Q_nm is (#58).** Archimedean piece (CST + Gauss of D₂ against
θ_nm) minus **one** atom w₂ θ(log 2). L fixed to log 3 in the first
scripts. Prime powers absent. Zero side absent (correct on the prime
side).

**What Q_window is (#66–#67).** Same archimedean piece at the new L,
minus ∑_{p<μ} w_p θ(log p). Still only *primes*, not p^k. At μ=5, n=4=2²
has log 4 < log 5. The Weil term Λ(4) χ(4) / √4 is missing (|weight| =
log 2 / 2 = 0.347). Higher 2^k until 2^k<μ too.

**What Weil’s Q is.** A − ∑_n Λ(n) χ(n) n^{-1/2} θ(log n) over all n
with log n ≤ L, plus the exact archimedean pairing (not only D₂ and a
CST fit). Zeros do not enter the prime- side writing.

So the 4-plane λ_min<0 on χ₃ at L=log 5 is a sign of *this* Q_window,
not yet a sign of Weil. Filling p^k and checking the archimedean CST
against the classical Γ-factor is the identification. A note, not a
harvest of γ.

**Judged (ql-arch-weil).** p^k is in Q. CST+Gauss on W_L equals the
digamma writing. The 4-plane λ_min +10^{-5} is still not the operator.
Neither Weil+ nor Weil−. Not RH.
