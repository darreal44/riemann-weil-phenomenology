# Spectral zeta functions

Usual object: for a positive operator (Laplacian on a compact manifold,
or a self-adjoint P > 0 with λ_n → ∞)

    ζ_P(s) = ∑_{n≥1} λ_n^{-s}

analytic continuation, ζ_P(0) related to det P via ζ'_P(0) = −log det P
(Ray– Singer, Minakshisundaram– Pleijel).

Selberg’s zeta is a different product over closed geodesics; its zeros
relate to the spectrum of the hyperbolic Laplacian (and to Maass forms
already in the repo). Not ζ_P.

On W_L we do not have {λ_n} of Q̂_L (`Wlog3-spectrum.md`). We have a
lower bound on λ_min for six χ at L=log 3, and a 4-plane sign at μ=5 for
a model form. That is not enough to write ζ_{Q̂}(s).

If Q̂_L were known positive with Weyl law λ_n ~ c n^α, then ζ_{Q̂} would
be a bookkeeping of the spectrum, not a proof of the sign of λ_min. The
class needs the bottom, not the meromorphic continuation of the sum of
reciprocals.

Riemann’s ζ is not the spectral zeta of Q̂_L. Under RH one can dream
that zeros γ are eigenvalues of some operator; that is Hilbert– Pólya,
another operator, already not constructed. Q̂_L positive is equivalent
to RH only when L and the test class are full Weil. A spectral zeta of a
truncated Q_nm would be a generating function of an approximation.

Not computed. Not a campaign.
