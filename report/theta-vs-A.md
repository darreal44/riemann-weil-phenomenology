# |θ_f(log 2)| versus A_L

f ∈ W_L, L=log 3,
y=log 2, y/L=log2/log3
≈ 0.6309.

θ_f(y) is the lag
quadratic form of f
at that y (the same
kernel that th_hat
discretises). A crude
L² bound

    |θ_f(y)| ≤ 2 ‖f‖₂²

is true and useless:
A_L(f) can be much
smaller than ‖f‖₂²
(the well). The ratio
that would take the
step is

    C_* = sup_{f≠0}
          |θ_f(log 2)| / A_L(f)

and the step on this
window, for a character
with w₂, is C_* |w₂| < 1
after the sign of w₂
is put in the right
place (χ₅: −w₂>0 so
the dangerous side is
θ negative).

C_* is an operator
norm: lag-evaluation
versus the
archimedean form,
on W_L. Bernstein
controls lag versus
L². Wrong metric.

Computing C_* on V_N
gives a decreasing
sequence bounded below
by the true C_*. That
is Courant again —
an upper bound on
nothing that proves
C_* |w₂|<1. A lower
bound on C_* is easy
(the witness). A
*upper* bound on C_*
is the missing line.
