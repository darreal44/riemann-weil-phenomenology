# Graph Fourier transform

GFT is the expansion in eigenvectors of a graph Laplacian. On the path
it is the DCT, i.e. the cosine ONB already in use. On a Cayley graph of
(Z/qZ)^× it is a character sum, which is already χ in the explicit
formula, not a new transform of θ.

There is no graph whose GFT coefficients are the v_n of the witness and
whose Parseval identity is Q. Writing one down would be Hilbert–Pólya in
disguise.

Not a campaign.
