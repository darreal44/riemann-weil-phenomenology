# Impact of H on Q(v)

Q = vᵀ H v, so
    ∂Q/∂H_{ij} = (2−δ_{ij}) v_i v_j.

χ₅ μ=16, v=(4,−3,1)/√26:

    entry    ∂Q/∂H
    H₀₀      +0.615
    H₁₁      +0.346
    H₂₂      +0.038
    H₀₁      −0.923
    H₀₂      +0.308
    H₁₂      −0.231

The sign of Q is most
sensitive to H₀₁
(coefficient −0.92) and
to H₀₀ (+0.62). That
matches the 0.133 vs
−0.123 split
(`Hoff.md`, `Hnn-impact.md`).

A 0.01 error on H₀₁
moves Q by 0.009 — more
than Q itself. The
enclosure of A(v) is
really an enclosure of
those two entries. H₂₂
barely counts (0.038).

This is the 3-plane at
one μ. Changing μ
changes H; the
derivatives above stay
the same only because v
is held fixed.
