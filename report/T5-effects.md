# Effects of T₅

Weight 0.720, edge lag at μ=5⁺, θ small (O(ε)).

    χ     χ(5)   effect on H near 5⁺
    χ₅     0     none
    χ₃    −1     +ΔH  (helps H₀₀)
    χ₇    −1     +ΔH
    χ₈    −1     +ΔH
    χ₁₇   −1     +ΔH
    χ₄    +1     −ΔH  (hurts H₀₀)

On χ₃ the help is +0.017 on H₀₀ at μ=5.1, maybe enough to lift a −10⁻⁴
well, not the cause of the well.

On χ₄ T₅ has the opposite sign and would push a take that was already
thin (+0.0002 at h=8, μ=5) down. Not run.

At larger ε, θ grows and T₅ becomes an ordinary interior lag like T₃ at
3.5 (weight even larger). The edge regime is short.

No effect at μ=5 exactly (`T5-on-H4.md`).
