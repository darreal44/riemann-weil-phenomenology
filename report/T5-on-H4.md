# T₅ on H₄

At μ=5, interior primes are {2,3}. p=5 is the endpoint, not interior.
Q_window does not include T₅ (#67, #68). So the measured H₄(μ=5) has
*zero* T₅.

At y=L, θ₀₀=2(L−y)/L =0. A lag exactly at the edge does not see the
constant mode. Other θ_nm(L) vanish the same way (compact support of the
test). Turning T₅ on at μ=5 exactly does nothing to H.

Just above 5, y=log 5 < L by a little, θ₀₀≈ 2ε/L small. ΔH₀₀ ≈ −w₅ χ(5)
· 2ε/L. χ₅ has χ(5)=0 anyway. χ₃ has χ(5)= kronecker(−3,5) = (5/−3) … =
+1 or −1. Either way the first order in ε is O(ε), while the crossing is
already done at ε=0 (H₄=−1.6×10⁻⁴ without T₅).

Effect of T₅ on H₄ at the crossing: none. After the crossing: a small
push whose sign is χ(5), not the reason μ* exists.
