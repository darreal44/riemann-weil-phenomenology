# T₅ modes on H₁₆, χ₃

At μ=5.1 T₅ turns on. #75: λ_min 8.27×10⁻⁶ → 6.10×10⁻⁶ (Δ=−2.2×10⁻⁶).
H₀₀ rises (χ₃(5)=−1, same sign as `T5-coefficients.md` on H₄).

So T₅ is positive definite on the constant mode and slightly negative on
the ground state of H₁₆. The bottom eigenvector is not φ₀. It is a mix
that sees the lag log 5 with the opposite weight to H₀₀ (off-diagonal
θ_{0n} and the already thin well from A(L)).

On H₄ the same T₅ was a +0.017 bump on every entry near the edge (almost
rank-one). On H₁₆ that rank-one update shifts sixteen modes. The ground
state, already orthogonal enough to φ₀, picks a tiny negative piece.

No 10⁻² jump: θ(log 5) is still O(ε) just above 5. The later drop to
10⁻⁹ at μ=7 is L and the drift of 5 into the window, not a new T₅ mode.

Not a Schrödinger bound state labelled T₅. It is one rank-one (almost)
update of the Gram.
