# Phase cancellation in ∫ r′ cos

r′ is smooth and slow on [0,L] (‖r′‖_∞≤0.071). cos(ω_k y) has k
oscillations. The integral is O(1/ω) by a second IPP, hence the
remainder after the first IPP is O(1/k²) and drops out of a 10^{−5}
table for k≥8.

There is no stationary point of the phase ω y on (0,L): the phase is
linear. Cancellation is plain Riemann–Lebesgue for a C¹ density, not a
stationary-phase miracle.
