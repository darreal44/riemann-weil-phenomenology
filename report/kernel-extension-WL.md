# Extension of the kernel past L

Q on W_L depends on
the kernel G only
through |y|≤L.
Any G~ with
G~|_{[-L,L]} = G|_{[-L,L]}
gives the same Q
on W_L. The cutoff
G=0 on |y|>L is
one extension. Its
symbol m_Q dips
below 0. That
does not forbid
another extension
whose symbol is
≥0. If one exists,
Bochner takes the
class: Q(f)=∫ρ ˆG~
with ˆG~≥0, ρ≥0.
Zero N. Operator
statement.

Constraints that
stay:

- The atom at
  ±log 2 lies
  *inside* (−L,L)
  (log 2 < log 3).
  It cannot be
  moved or erased.
  Every extension
  still subtracts
  w₂ cos(t log 2).
- The continuous
  piece on [0,L]
  (Laplace / D₂
  truncated) is
  fixed. Only the
  tail y>L is
  free.

**Pólya.** An even
continuous φ,
convex and
decreasing to 0
on [0,∞), is
positive definite.
If the continuous
part of G on
[0,L] *plus a
tail* can be made
even, convex,
↓0, then that
tail is a
certificate.
The atom ±log 2
is not convex;
it sits in the
fixed piece. A
Pólya tail can
only rescue the
continuous
symbol. It does
not cancel a
negative w₂.

**Krein.**
Trigonometric
moment problem
on the interval:
does the
restriction of G
to [−L,L] admit
*some* PD
extension to ℝ?
Equivalent to
positive
definiteness of
the restriction
as a kernel on
the interval
(Toeplitz on
delays ≤L).
That is the
band question
named twice.
A constructive
Krein extension
(if the moment
problem is
determinate or
a Pick
function is
found) is a
proof. An
abstract
existence
without a
symbol ≥0 in
hand is not
yet Bochner
taking the
class.

What #57
killed: one
extension (the
cutoff). What
it did not
kill: the
existence of
another.
Cosines stay
the necessary
family on W_L.
Pólya tail /
Krein moment
on [−L,L] is
the sufficient
route with
zero N.
