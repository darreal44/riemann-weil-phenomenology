# Deligne, Weil II

P. Deligne,
La conjecture de Weil : II,
Publ. Math. IHÉS 52 (1980).

Weil I (1974)
is RH for
H^i of a
smooth
projective
variety
over F_q:
eigenvalues
of Frob
have
absolute
value
q^{i/2}.

Weil II

cohomology
with

in a

ℓ-adic
sheaf,

A lisse

pointwise

weight w

closed

eigenvalues
of Frob_x

|α| =
N(x)^{w/2}.
Mixed =
extensions

The

lisse
pure of
weight w

smooth
U, then
H^i_c
is mixed
of
weights
≤ w+i,
and
equals
w+i
when
the
support
is
proper
smooth
(purity).
