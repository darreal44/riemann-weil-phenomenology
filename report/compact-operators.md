s_n(K)→0). A compression to the tail n≥N then has norm →0 as N→∞.

tail stays π. That is why raising N_NEAR does not kill Off_far’s ½π.

(|a_n| keeps hitting ~0.29). It is not compact either. ‖B₁‖ staying
