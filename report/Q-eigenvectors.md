## Reading

The well and its partner
are the only eigenvectors
with PR ≈ 2 and ‖head‖²≈1.
Everything else is a tail
mode with PR 2–5, peak
walking outward. No
hidden localized state
in T: those would show
PR≈1 on a high hat and
a small λ, and they
don’t appear until λ~1.
