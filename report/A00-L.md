# A₀₀ does not go to −∞ like −L/2

χ₅, φ₀ only.

    μ     L      CST     A₀₀    A₀₀/L   A₀₀+L/2
    6    1.79   −0.084  −0.79   −0.440    0.107
   16    2.77   −0.109  −1.38   −0.499    0.003
   23    3.14   −0.111  −1.55   −0.495    0.015
   37    3.61   −0.112  −1.75   −0.483    0.060
   50    3.91   −0.112  −1.85   −0.474    0.102

A₀₀/L *peaks* at μ=16
(−0.499) and comes back.
A₀₀+L/2 is a U with a
minimum at μ=16. CST has
already frozen (−0.112).

The integrand is
D₂(y)(2 e^{−(2−2s₀)y} − θ₀₀(y))
with D₂ ∼ e^{−y/2} /
(1−e^{−2y}). Mass lives
at small y. Extending L
adds a tail, not a fresh
L/2. So A₀₀ → A₀₀(∞),
a constant, not −∞.

The −cL reading of A(v)
on μ=6..37 was a mid-window
slope, not the limit.
Once A saturates, Q = A−P
is a constant minus a
prime sum that may itself
saturate (θ(log p) for
p≪μ is already in).
That changes the (∀ μ)
problem: it is no longer
“two series to −∞, keep
the gap”, it is “two
convergent objects, keep
the sign of the limit”.
