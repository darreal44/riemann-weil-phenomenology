# H₀₀ versus H₀₁

Atom lifts, χ(2)=−1,
closed form:

    ΔH₀₀ = −w₂ θ₀₀(log 2) = +0.362
    ΔH₀₁ = −w₂ θ₀₁(log 2) = +0.162

θ₀₀/θ₀₁ = 0.738/0.330
        = 2.24
= ΔH₀₀/ΔH₀₁.

The constant
mode sees more
of the lag
(log 2 is not
a node of φ₀).
φ₀–φ₁ sees
less (one
oscillation).

After the lift:

          H00    H01    H01/H00
    χ₈   0.311   0.324   1.04
    χ₄   0.154   0.318   2.07
    χ₅   0.202   0.485   2.40
    χ₃   0.228   0.480   2.11

Without atom,
H01/H00 is
already >1
on the odd
side (χ₄).
The atom
pushes χ₅
to 2.40.
χ₃ sits
with χ₄
in the
ratio, and
loses on
H11 instead.

So H₀₀ vs
H₀₁ is not
“which is
bigger”
(H01 > H00
on three of
four). It
is how much
of H00 H11
remains
after H01²
(`atom-on-det.md`).
