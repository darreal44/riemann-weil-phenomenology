# Quantum chaos

Bohigas–Giannoni–Schmit: spectra of quantized chaotic
systems follow GUE/GOE. Berry–Tabor: integrable systems
follow Poisson. The zeros of ζ (and of Dirichlet L) agree
with GUE to the accuracy of the Odlyzko lists. That is the
only quantum-chaos statement this repository uses, and it
is a statement about Γ, not about spec(Q).

## What we do not have

- A classical billiard or geodesic flow whose quantization
  is Q. Q is a Gram of evaluations of a window basis at Γ.
- A Gutzwiller trace formula for spec(Q). The explicit
  formula we started from *is* a trace formula, but for
  the zeros, with periodic orbits = primes. That is Weil,
  already assembled.
- A spectral form factor of the eigenvalues of Q. N=33
  and one isolated λ0 do not make a form factor.

Berry–Keating (xp Hamiltonian) is a model for the zeros,
not for v0. The charged pair being tight is a local GUE
fluctuation, already allowed (`anderson-1d-rmt.md`).

## Status

Chaos in this project = GUE of Γ. It does not relocate
the singlet and does not prove c_L ≥ e^{-C τ |E|}.
