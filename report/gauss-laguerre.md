# Gauss–Laguerre

Gauss–Laguerre integrates f(x) e^{−x} on [0,∞). The Bose piece
2e^{−3y/2} looks like that weight after x=3y/2. The finite cutoff y=1 or
y=L and the θ_v oscillation kill the pure Laguerre setting: the
integrand of a is not a polynomial times e^{−3y/2} on the half-line.

A truncated Laguerre rule on [0,1] is a weighted Gauss rule with the
wrong interval. It would again score I_true, not build g_lo.

I_{[1,L]} is already handled by a Cauchy floor, not by a half-line
quadrature.

Not a campaign.
