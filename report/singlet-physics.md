# Physics of the singlet (v0 on the charged pair)

## Composition

On the Gram, v0 is *odd* across the charged pair: Φ(γ_a)·v0 ≈ −Φ(γ_b)·v0.
χ₂₉ μ=22: +0.024 and −0.022 on (10.42, 11.20), 61 % of the mass.
χ₁₇ μ=22: −0.001 and +0.001 on (15.64, 16.27), 43 %.
The rest (39–57 %) is a smooth tail on other zeros — the screening
cloud in frequency.

v1 on χ₂₉ is *even* on the desert (1.79, 5.32), λ = 1.2. That is the
cheap bonding combination of a wide gap: no node forced between the
two points. The expensive object is the node squeezed into a gap
0.38 ν.

## Binding

A sign change on an interval of length δ costs type. The Paley–Wiener
budget is τ = L/2; spending it on a node in δ gives a factor
roughly exp(−c τ δ) from a local Bernstein / rolling estimate, which
is *not* the measured ell (χ₂₉: τδ ≈ 0.60, e^{-0.6} is nothing).
The exponential we do measure is the global isolation of this odd
state from the rest of the window: λ1/λ0 = 330 already at μ=22,
10^4 at μ=38. The singlet is bound in *index*, not by a local
exp(−τδ).

## Overlap Q / Gram

Same N: the Q-mode and the Gram-mode overlap at > 0.99 on the
dumped windows (computed with matching dimension). The prime-side
singlet is the zero-side odd pair state.

## Screening

“Screened” means: no other small eigenvalue sits on that pair.
v1 has already left it (desert, even, O(1)). Further harmonics
pick other pairs. The pair is locked into one odd combination and
drops out of the low spectrum — the Kondo meaning of screening
here.

μ=11 on χ₂₉: λ1/λ0 = 6, the odd pair state is only half-isolated.
That is an incomplete singlet, not a second channel.
