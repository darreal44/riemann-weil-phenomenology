# Isolation vs μ: crossover, not a jump

log10(λ₁/λ₀) on the Gram, N=29.

| μ | χ₂₉ | χ₁₇ | χ₁₉ |
|---|-----|-----|-----|
| 8 | 0.31 | 1.64 | 0.48 |
| 11 | 0.79 | 2.23 | 0.86 |
| 16 | 1.39 | 3.60 | 1.87 |
| 20 | 2.19 | 4.62 | 2.51 |
| 22 | 2.52 | 4.73 | 3.14 |
| 30 | 3.25 | 6.00 | 4.22 |
| 38 | 4.40 | 6.66 | 5.12 |
| 50 | 4.92 | 6.68 | 5.97 |

After μ ≈ 16–20 the log-gap rises almost linearly in μ: λ₁/λ₀ ∼ exp(αμ)
with α ≈ 0.2–0.3. That is a *crossover scale*, not a discontinuity.

Define μ* by λ₁/λ₀ = 100 (log10 = 2): χ₁₇ ≈ 10, χ₂₉ ≈ 20, χ₁₉ ≈ 18.
Below μ* the singlet is incomplete (χ₂₉ at 11). Above, it is isolated
and C has frozen. ell itself has no kink at μ* — only the gap does.

No second crossing appears up to 50. No two-channel phase. The
“transition” is the Kondo crossover of one pair, rounded over
Δμ ≈ 6–8.
