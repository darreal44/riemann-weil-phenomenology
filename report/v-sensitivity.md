# Sensitivity of Q to v

χ₅ μ=16, 3-plane.
v = (4, −3, 1)/√26, Q=0.00554.

On the sphere,
    ∇Q = 2 (H − Q I) v
    ‖∇Q‖ = 0.107
    ‖∇Q‖ / Q ≈ 19

A 1% move of v changes
Q by ~20% relatively
(+0.0004 to +0.0015
on the three axes).
Sign does not flip.

    v + 0.01 e₀   Q=0.0059
    v + 0.01 e₁   Q=0.0063
    v + 0.01 e₂   Q=0.0063
    (4,−3,1.1)    Q=0.0070

Hessian 2(H−QI) has
eigenvalues
    −0.0055, −0.0033, +0.517

Two flat directions
(the well) and one
stiff bulk axis (φ₂
mix). Sensitivity is
high *relatively*
because Q itself is
5×10^{-3}; it is small
*absolutely* (10^{-4}
per percent of v).

This is why the pencil
(4,−3,*) all witnesses
(`v-impact.md`) and why
a hand enclosure of A(v)
needs three digits.
