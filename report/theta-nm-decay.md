(`theta_hat`):

    n=m=0:
      2(L−y)/L
      (no
      decay
      in

    n=0, m≥1:
      |θ|
      ≤ √2 /(π m)
      (times
      a
      bounded
      sin).
    n=m≥1:
      O(1)
      +
      O(1/n)

      (L−y)/L
      cos

      does
      not

      n).
    n≠m,
      both

      |θ|
      ≤ 2 max(n,m) / (π |m²−n²|)
      = O(1 / |n−m|)
      after
      |m²−n²|=|m−n||m+n|.
