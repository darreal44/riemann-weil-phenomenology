Δ=−0.44, ΔL=1.14, ≈ −0.39.
Slope of A(v) on the same
interval: −0.46 / 1.14 ≈
−0.40. Matched to 3%.

2 and 3 sliding with L
*is* the tracking of P
against A after μ=16.
New primes are a 0.002
ripple on top. A proof
that Q stays positive
is a proof that this
pair (P₂(L)+P₃(L)) does
not outrun A(v;L) once
the e^{−y/2} tail of A
has flattened.
