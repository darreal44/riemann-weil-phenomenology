# H₃₃ = A₃₃ − P₃₃

χ₅ μ=16, n=m=3.

    θ₃₃(y) = 2( (L−y)/L cos(6π y/L)
                − sin(6π y/L)/(6π) )
    θ₃₃(0)=2,  θ₃₃(L)=0,  θ₃₃(L/2)=−1

    A₃₃ = F₀/2 · CST + ½ ∫ D₂ (F₀ e^{−(2−2s₀)y} − θ₃₃)
        = 1.771
        CST = −0.109  (small)

    P₃₃ = ∑_{n≤16} χ(n) Λ(n) n^{−1/2} θ₃₃(log n)
        = −1.242

    H₃₃ = 1.771 − (−1.242) = 3.013

Almost every prime power
lands negative (θ₃₃(log n)
and χ₅ Λ often the same
sign). P₃₃ is a negative
O(1), so −P₃₃ *adds* to
the already positive A₃₃.

That is why φ₃ is bulk
and H₃₃≈3: archimedean
mass ~1.8 plus a prime
sum that does not cancel
it. Contrast H₀₀=0.092,
where A₀₀=−1.38 and
P₀₀=−1.47 cancel.
