# Courant–Fischer on H_n

For a
self-adjoint
operator
Q̂
(bounded
below)
on
a
Hilbert
space,

    λ_min(Q̂)
      = inf_{‖v‖=1} ⟨Q̂ v, v⟩

If
V_n ⊂ V_{n+1}
are
finite
dimensional
subspaces
and
H_n
is
Q̂
restricted
to
V_n
(the
cosine
head),

    λ_min(H_n)
      = min_{v∈V_n, ‖v‖=1} ⟨Q̂ v, v⟩
      ≥ λ_min(Q̂)

and
n ↦ λ_min(H_n)
is
decreasing.
That
is
Courant
“from
above”:
every
finite
section
is
an
*upper*
bound
on
the
true
bottom.
A
positive
H_n
does
not
prove
Q̂≥0.
A
negative
H_n
proves
Q̂
is
not
≥0
(on
that
space,
for
that
form).

#74
is
the
monotone
chain
16→24→32→48,
all
positive,
all
above
c_L^*.
#73
is
why
Neumann
exists:
to
get
a
*lower*
bound
that
can
take
the
class
inside
one
L.
When
ρ≥1
that
lower
bound
dies;
Courant
alone
cannot
replace
it.

in
the
journal
was
the
step
(log 2, log 3]:
the
PW
step
is
not

because
a
section
can
be
positive
while
the
class
