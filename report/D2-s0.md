# D₂(s₀) in detail

    D₂(y,s₀) = 2 e^{−2 s₀ y} / (1 − e^{−2y}) = e^{(1−2 s₀) y} / sinh(y)

Series (y>0):

    D₂ = 2 ∑_{k≥0} e^{−2(s₀+k) y}

Two useful limits:

    y→0⁺ : D₂ ∼ 1/y (independent of s₀)
    y→∞ : D₂ ∼ 2 e^{−2 s₀ y}

Ratio of the two cells:

    D₂(y, 3/4) / D₂(y, 1/4) = e^{−y}

At L=log 3 the odd kernel is three times smaller at the right endpoint
than the even one (e^{−log 3}=1/3), and almost equal near 0.

G_reg = D₂ − 1/y is finite at 0 and negative for s₀=3/4 in a
neighbourhood of 0 (the χ₃ well of A). For s₀=1/4 G_reg(0⁺) is higher.

D₂ E_C = 2/(e^{2y}−1) kills s₀ (`EC-regularisation.md`). The s₀
dependence that survives in A is only in −½∫ D₂ θ and in ψ(s₀) inside
the constant.

Not a new kernel. This is the same D₂ as in Q_nm.
