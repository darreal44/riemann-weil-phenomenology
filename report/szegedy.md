# Szegedy

Szegedy lifts a reversible Markov chain P to a unitary on ℓ²(E) whose
spectrum is determined by that of the discriminant D=√(ΠP). Hitting and
search speedups follow when P has a gap.

The lab has no Markov chain on a state space of zeros or primes whose
discriminant is Q̂. T_y is a deterministic shift on an interval, not a
stochastic P.
