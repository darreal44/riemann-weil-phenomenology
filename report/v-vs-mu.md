Positive at every window
in this list. Not monotone
to 0: a dip at μ=16, then
a rise. λ_min(H₃) sits on
the floor for μ≥16 — the
3-plane well is resolved
as "zero" in float64
while Q(v) stays O(10^{-2}).

This is not (∀ L) Q_L(v)≥0.
It is five points. The
vector was chosen at μ=16;
it happens to remain a
witness on either side.
A proof for all μ would
have to track A(μ)−P(μ)
on this fixed combination
of three hats, including
through every new prime
that crosses μ.
