# Hilbert spectrum of G — χ₂₉ μ=11, N=24, T=320

G acts on the 25-dimensional Hilbert space of
cosine hats. Eigenvalues of that operator:

    λ = 0.298, 1.749, 2.593, … , 7.438

| piece | count | range |
|---|---|---|
| well | 1 | 0.298 |
| first excited | 1 | 1.749 |
| bulk | 23 | 2.59–7.44 |
| negative | 0 | — |

tr G = 116, ‖G‖_HS = 24.6, ‖G‖_op = 7.44,
mean 4.64.

## Reading

This is not Hilbert–Pólya (those eigenvalues
would be the γ's). It is the spectrum of the
Weil / Gram form on a Paley–Wiener window.

The well is isolated by a gap of factor ~6
to λ₁. Courant (#35) already said the 2-plane
cannot see this: λ_min of a 2-face sits in
the bulk. The Hilbert-space picture is one
bound state plus a gapped continuum of O(1)
modes — the T>0 block of the Schur split.

HS/op ≈ 3.3 is the effective dimension of
the bulk (√23 ~ 4.8 would be white; the
spectrum is peaked high, not flat).

Raising T only moves λ₀ (~1.4/T). λ₁ and
the bulk are already frozen at T=69.
