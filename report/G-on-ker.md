# ‖G|_ker‖ is not small

ker = ker(Eval_ω* | V(ω*)),
dim = K* on the windows
where the SVD is clean.

    χ     K*  dimker  ‖G|ker‖    C=−ln‖‖   #{ℓ>2} on ker
    χ₅    5   5       3.42       −1.23     3
    χ₁₃   3   3       3.67       −1.30     1

C is *negative*: some
unit kernel vectors have
out-of-cut mass O(1).
The hypothesis

    ‖G|_ker‖ ≤ e^{−2}

fails by two orders of
magnitude. Min-max
therefore does *not*
give #{ℓ>2} ≥ K*.

What min-max does give,
on this ker, is
#{ℓ>2} = 3 (χ₅) and 1
(χ₁₃) — matching the
observed well-count at
cut 2, smaller than K*.

The extra kernel
directions live at ℓ ≤ 2
(χ₅: 5−3=2 directions,
one of them at ℓ>½).
That is the plunge,
seen from inside the
kernel rather than from
the full Gram.

Closing the
threshold-free count
means proving those
extra directions still
have ℓ > 2 after all,
or lowering K* to the
well-count. Neither
follows from Rayleigh
alone.
