# Weil-positivity

Classical: Q(θ) ≥ 0 for every admissible even test θ (or every L>0 and
every θ in W_L with the right decay). That statement, for one χ, is GRH
for L(s,χ). For ζ, RH.

In this repo a weaker word was used too often. The dictionary after
#71–#80:

**Weil-positive** = the classical statement above, A identified with
A_∞, every n with log n < L kept, every θ in the class, every L. Never
claimed.

**Take** = S_lo>0 for one χ, one L, T infinite, cap on ‖Θ‖. A lower
bound on one window, not Weil-positive.

**Section +** = λ_min(H_n)>0. Courant from above. Not a take, not
Weil-positive.

**SURVIVE** = the prereg matched the output. Orthogonal to positivity.

A negative section of the *identified* form would be Weil-negative for
that θ, hence a disproof of GRH for that χ — if θ is admissible for the
explicit formula. No such section was found on the identified writing
(#71 mixed writing does not count).
