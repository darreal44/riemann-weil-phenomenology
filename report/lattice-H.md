# Lattice form of H

H at χ₅ μ=16 is a
positive definite
quadratic form on
ℝ³, hence on the
hat lattice ℤ³.
Short integer
vectors (raw Q =
n² Q_unit):

    v           Q_raw   Q_unit
    ±(3,−2,0)   0.0015  0.00012
    ±(4,−4,1)   0.0046  0.00014
    ±(0,−1,1)   0.0056  0.00279
    ±(3,−3,1)   0.0068  0.00036
    ±(4,−3,1)   larger than these

The Minkowski-short
direction is
(3,−2,0), i.e. the
same plane as
(4,−3,0): the
middle mode λ₁.
The human witness
(4,−3,1) is not a
successive minimum.
It was chosen to
sit on λ₂ as well
(α₂≠0), so Q_unit
is larger and more
stable
(`alpha-1-2.md`).

This is just ℤ³
with the matrix H.
Not D₄, not a
root lattice, not
a modular form of
weight 12. The
word “lattice”
stops at the hat
coefficients.

det H > 0
(`detH-3hats.md`)
is the covolume
factor of that
form, not a
height pairing.
