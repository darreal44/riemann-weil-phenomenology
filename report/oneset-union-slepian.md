## Consequence

The one-set minorant cannot be

    ell ≤ −log(1−λ_max(K_E)).

That dies the moment E has two extra
pieces. The remaining statement is the
sampling one: zeros on Eᶜ form a stable
set for PW_τ|Eᶜ, with a constant A
independent of the number of pieces.
That A is still not derived.
