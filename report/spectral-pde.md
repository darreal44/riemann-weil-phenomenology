unknown
in

global
basis
(Chebyshev,
Fourier)
and
collocates
or
Galerkin-projects
a
PDE.

lab
already
does
the
Galerkin
half
on
W_L
with
the
cosine
ONB.
There
is
no
PDE
whose
unknown
is
g
or
Q̂.

Collocation
of
a
differential
equation
for
θ
or
for
g
would
need
an
equation.
We
have
closed
forms
for
θ_nm
and
a
quadrature
for
a.
A
tau
method
or
a
Chebyshev
collocation
of
g''=…
is
weaker
than
the
supporting
parabolas
already
used
on
[0,1].

Not
a
campaign.
