# Short holes (gaps ≤ ν)

These are the complementary set S = R_+ \ E_L. They are not hiding
places. They are the sampling points of PW_{L/2}.

Most consecutive zeros are short holes. Narrow deserts have more
of them (χ₂₉). Mean short gap is ~ ν/2.

## Effect on sampling

A short gap is two zeros closer than Nyquist. Locally redundant:
the two point evaluations are coherent. Duffin–Schaeffer still
holds on S because the gaps are *bounded above* by ν, which is
exactly the Nyquist length. Short holes make the sampling
*stabler*, not weaker. They do not cost an exponential in |gap|.

## Effect on v0

One of them is the charged pair (χ₂₉: 0.78 = 0.38 ν). The odd
combination on that pair is the singlet. The other 300 short
gaps share the 40 % cloud and the O(1) harmonics. Tightness
alone does not pick the pair (`gram-profile.md`).

## Effect on ell / C

Short holes are absent from |E|, so they do not enter
C L |E| as length. They enter as the *place* where the unique
slow mode can lock. Without at least one short gap in the
window band, there is no odd node to pay, and the floor would
be a desert-Slepian (much shallower). The short hole that
matters is one; the rest are sampling furniture.

## One line

Long holes hide. Short holes sample. One short hole binds v0.
