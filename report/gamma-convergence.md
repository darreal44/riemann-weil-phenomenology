# Convergence of the Γ panels

Each Γ_R(s+μ) contributes, on the window [0,L],

    D2(y) ∝ e^{-2 s0 y} / (1−e^{-2y}),    s0 = 1/4 + μ/2.

## Weight 2 (μ=0,1)

s0=1/4 and 3/4. Both decay as e^{-y/2} and e^{-3y/2}.
On L=log 38≈3.64 they still see the whole interval.
CST = log(N/π)−γ − log(1−e^{-2L}) → log(N/π)−γ.
The cutoff vanishes. That is the only “convergence” of Γ:
the finite-L edge term dies, the bulk integral stays O(1).

## Weight 12 (μ=0,11)

s0=1/4 and 23/4=5.75. The second kernel is e^{-11.5 y}.
At y=0.5 already e^{-5.75}≈0.003. Effectively a jet at y=0,
independent of L as soon as L≳1. Raising μ does not
“converge” that panel — it was already local.

The first panel (s0=1/4) is the same as even Dirichlet and
does grow with the window in the usual way.

## What this says about Q_Δ

λ0=−2.39 at μ=11 is not a transient of small L. The heavy
Γ_R(s+11) is already in its L=∞ shape. Changing μ will
not flip the sign by “waiting for Γ to converge.” If Q_Δ
is indefinite, the mismatch is the CST / the n^{-6} weight
/ the missing (2π)^{-s} piece, not a cutoff in L.

No extra Δ scan until that local term is derived from
Γ'/Γ(6+it), not from copying two Dirichlet CSTs.
