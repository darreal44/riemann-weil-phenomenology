# Anderson localization versus Q

Anderson: a random Schrödinger operator on ℓ²(Z^d), almost-sure
pure point spectrum, eigenfunctions e^{-|x-x0|/ξ}. Mobility edge
if a region of extended states survives.

## What looks similar

v0 is localized in the Fourier basis of the window (N_eff≈1.7,
70 % on a0) and in frequency on one pair. That is a localized
ground state of a *deterministic* Gram. The “disorder” is the
irregular spacing of Γ.

Higher eigenvectors spread (k̄ = 6–22). A naive reading is
localized ground state, extended bulk. The jump λ0 ≪ 1 → λ1=O(1)
is a gap, not a mobility edge: there is no parameter that
closes it besides the finite-μ crossover already measured
(λ1/λ0 : 6 → 10⁶).

## What is missing

- No random ensemble. Γ is fixed. One sample is not Anderson.
- No infinite-volume limit. N = 33–67. Every vector is ℓ².
- No Lyapunov exponent, no fractional-moment bound, no
  Wegner estimate.
- Q is a Gram of evaluations, not −Δ + V_ω.

A localization length ξ extracted from |v0,n| ~ e^{-n/ξ} is
meaningless at N_eff=2 (only two coefficients).

## Status

The word “localized” for v0 is allowed as Fourier-sparse.
Anderson theory is not in play. The gap λ1/λ0 is the Kondo
crossover of the singlet, already written. No mobility edge
to hunt on these matrices.
