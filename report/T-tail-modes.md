# Tail modes of T — χ₅

T = Q restricted to hats φ₃, φ₄, …
PR = 1/∑ wᵢ⁴ (1 = one hat, N = flat).

## Spectra side by side

μ=8
    Q  10⁻⁶, 0.027, 1.04, 1.96 … 3.64
    T        1.07, 2.25, 2.39 … 3.61

μ=16
    Q  10⁻¹³, 3×10⁻⁸, 1×10⁻³, 0.35, 1.56 … 4.15
    T                   0.23, 1.43, 1.86 … 4.04

μ=22
    Q  10⁻¹⁷, 5×10⁻¹², 4×10⁻⁷, 8×10⁻⁴, 0.47, 1.46 … 4.41
    T                          5×10⁻⁴, 0.32, 1.44 … 4.41

The well and the next two
small eigenvalues of Q are
*absent* from T. They live
in the head (previous note).
T first sees Q at the fourth
eigenvalue: 8×10⁻⁴ ≈ 5×10⁻⁴
at μ=22.

## Where T's modes sit

Lowest T mode, localized
(PR 1.3–2.7), walks outward:

    μ=8   φ₅ (0.72)
    μ=16  φ₆ (0.93)
    μ=22  φ₇ (0.77)

The rest of T is bulk
[1.4, 4.4], PR 2–6, no
structure. Ordinary hat
oscillations of type L.

## Reading

T is “the quadratic form
on functions that already
used their three lowest
frequencies”. Its small
mode is the next Slepian
that the window can
support, not the well.
The well used φ₀ and φ₁
and never entered T.
