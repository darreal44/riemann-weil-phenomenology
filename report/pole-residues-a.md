# Residues of a at kπi

    a = ½ w g
    Res(a; kπi) = ½ Res(w) g(kπi)

Res(w) is ±1 or ±i.
g = 2e^{−3z/2} − θ_v
at z=kπi is huge:
θ_v contains
cos(ω n z) =
cosh(ω n k π)
with ω=2π n/L
and L=log 16≈2.77,
so ωπ ≈ 2.27π ≈ 7.1
already at n=1,k=1.

    k     |Res a|   (order)
    ±1    4×10^4
    ±2    1×10^11
    ±3    2×10^17

A residue series
for ∫_ℝ a is not
a method: the
terms explode,
and the integral
is only on [0,L]
anyway. Closing
a large contour
picks up those
terms plus an
arc that does
not vanish.

#52/#53 never
sum residues.
They stay inside
|Im z|<2 < π and
bound |a|, hence
|a^{(6)}|. The
useful datum at
the pole is the
*location*, not
the residue.
