# Gutzwiller trace formula

Gutzwiller:
the
density
of
states
of
a
quantum
Hamiltonian
is
the
mean
Weyl
term
plus
a
sum
over
classical
periodic
orbits
(action,
period,
stability
amplitude).
It
is
a
semi-classical
trace
formula
for
−Δ+V
or
a
billiard,
not
a
theorem
for
Q̂_L.

Selberg
is
the
rigorous
cousin
on
a
hyperbolic
surface
(orbits
= closed
geodesics,
spectrum
= Laplace
/ Maass).
Weil
is
the
arithmetic
cousin
(orbits
= primes,
spectrum
= zeros).
The
formal
shape
“Tr
= mean
+ periodic
sum”
is
the
same
slogan.

Berry–Keating
and
the
Hilbert–Pólya
dream
want
a
Hamiltonian
whose
Gutzwiller
sum
*is*
the
explicit
formula
(primes
as
orbits).
That
Hamiltonian
is
not
Q̂_L
(Q̂
is
already
the
quadratic
form
built
from
the
explicit
formula,
not
a
−Δ+V
whose
trace
would
reproduce
it).

No
periodic-orbit
sum
is
computed
here.
Primes
in
P
are
the
orbits
of
Weil,
already
in
the
matrix.
