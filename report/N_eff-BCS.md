# N_eff, BCS, and where it stops

N_eff = 1 / Σ p_n², p_n = |v0,n|² in the cosine basis.
= 1 : one bar. = 2 : two bars equally. ≫2 : the mode
has dissolved into the bulk.

## What we measured

| object | N_eff | λ1/λ0 | verdict |
|--------|-------|-------|---------|
| χ₂₉ Q μ=22–38 | 1.7 | 10²–10⁶ | pair |
| χ₃₁ Q μ=38 | 1.29 | 3e3 | pair, almost a0 |
| 11a1 Gram μ=22–38 | 2.1–2.3 | 10⁵ | pair |
| 67a1 Gram μ=38 | 1.75 | 3e3 | pair, shallow |
| χ₅ Q μ=38 | 2.46 | large | pair + one extra bar |
| 11a1 Q μ=38 | 4.43 | 0.73 | **not a pair** |

When λ1/λ0 drops below ~10, N_eff climbs and the “pair”
is no longer isolated. That is the 11a1 prime-side Q at
μ=38 (the panel that went negative).

## BCS dictionary (only that)

BCS: a two-body bound state, gap Δ, occupation ~2.
Here: v0 supported on two Fourier bars, gap = log(λ1/λ0).
N_eff is the analogue of the pair occupancy, not of the
density of states.

BCS also has a critical temperature where Δ→0 and the
pair breaks. The analogue we already named is the μ*
crossover of λ1/λ0, not a thermodynamic T_c. There is
no chemical potential, no phonon glue, no Fermi surface.

## Limit of validity

- N_eff ∈ [1.3, 2.5] **and** λ1/λ0 > 10² : the word “pair”
  is allowed as a count of bars.
- N_eff > 3 or λ1/λ0 < 10 : do not say BCS. The 11a1 Q at
  μ=38 is in that bin (and λ0<0).
- Gram χ₅ indefinite : no N_eff at all.
- BCS gap equation, Tc, isotope effect : not in the repo.

N_eff does not drive ell. ell is set by |E| / C(χ).
N_eff only says whether the ground vector is still two
coefficients while that ell is measured.
