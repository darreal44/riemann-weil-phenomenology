# Vote correlations

A vote is the sign of
drop-p. Correlation
here means: do two
primes cross 0 in the
same window, and does
one’s vote predict
the other’s.

Judged pairs, 37a1:

    μ     drop-3    drop-83   drop-71
   80    +0.089     mute      in-sum,
                              plateau lived
   84    +0.048    +3e-9      (not dropped)
  100    −0.418    −0.080     (not dropped)

3 and 83 flip in the
*same* interval
(84,100]. That is
one co-occurrence,
not a coefficient.
The amplitudes are
not proportional
(−0.42 vs −0.08).
83’s arrival moved
drop-3 by 0.04 and
did not flip it;
the flip of 3
happened later,
with 83 already
coupling.

71 at 80: present,
heavy, edge, and
drop-3 stayed +.
Heavy-edge at t
does not imply a
joint flip at t.

What would look
like correlation:
a grid μ=86,88,…
with both drops.
If they cross at
the same μ*, the
ground state is
one direction that
needs both terms.
If 3 flips first,
83 is a follower.
If 83 flips first,
3 is riding 83’s
mode. Three points
cannot tell.

So: co-window, not
co-threshold. Not
a correlation
matrix. The
taxonomy stays
per (p, μ).
