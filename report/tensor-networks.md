# Tensor networks

An MPS of bond dimension χ on the cosine chain of length N
would represent v0 if the Schmidt ranks across cuts stay ≤χ.

v0 has N_eff≈2 and weight on n=0,1,2 only. Across any cut
that separates {0,1,2} from the tail, the Schmidt rank is 1
(the tail is ≈0). Bond dimension χ=1: a product state with
a two- or three-site block on the left. That is the same
sentence as N_eff≈2.

A PEPS or MERA on the zeros would need a 2D lattice we do
not have. Γ is a 1D point set. DMRG on Q is exact diagonalization
at N=37.

No tensor is built. The network language restates the
already dumped mode.
