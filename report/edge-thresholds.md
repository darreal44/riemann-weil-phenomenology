a theorem.

83 leaves “edge” only
around μ ≈ 105 (y/L=0.95
→ μ=83^{1/0.95}≈105).
It is already necessary
at 100, still edge.
Necessary ∩ edge is
not empty. The 0.95
cut does not track
the vote.

3 is never edge on
these windows
(y/L~0.25). Its
threshold is
interior, not
border.

71 at μ=80 is as
edge as 83 at 100
and did not vote
then. Edge + heavy
is not a sufficient
rule (`term-83.md`).

A sharper edge
would be θ₀₀ itself
(0.08 at 83@100 vs
0.006 at arrival).
That moved a factor
13 while the drop-83
went from +10⁻⁹ to
−0.08. The coupling
to the ground state
grew faster than
θ₀₀. The border
coordinate y/L is
too coarse for the
sign.
