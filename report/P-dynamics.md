# Dynamics of P(v)

χ₅, v = (4, −3, 1)/√26.

P(μ=50) = −1.296, 21 terms.
By n-band:

    [2, 3]    −1.345
    [4, 8]    −0.052
    [9,16]    +0.115
    [17,32]   −0.011
    [33,50]   −0.005

2 and 3 *are* P. After
n=16 the rest is 1%.

A new prime at μ=p,
evaluated just after the
edge (L=log(1.1 p)):

    |ΔP| ≈ 0.0014–0.0023
    sign = χ₅(p)

Alternating, shrinking
like (log p)/√p × O(θ)
with θ(v)∼0.003 near
the edge.

So the motion of P after
μ=16 is almost entirely
the *old* terms 2,3,4,…
moving with L (θ(log n, L)
depends on L), plus a
±0.002 shot each time a
prime appears. There is
no drift of P from new
primes that could eat Q
= 0.003. The risk is
2 and 3 sliding with L
faster than A.

That slide is the same
tracking as A (`Av-Pv-track.md`).
P is not a random walk
of kicks.
