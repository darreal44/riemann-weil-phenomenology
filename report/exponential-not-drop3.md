# Exponential model

The *full* form is
compatible with an
exponential well:

    μ     L      λ₀         ℓ₀=−ln λ₀    ℓ₀/L
   62    4.13   5.26e-7     14.46        3.50
   74    4.30   4.97e-8     16.82        3.91
   80    4.38   2.85e-8     17.37        3.96

ℓ₀ grows with L
(roughly 3.5–4 per unit
L on this scrap). That
is the same scale as
the desert/Slepian
reading of the *Gram*
wells, by coincidence
of numbers, not
identity of operators.

Drop-3 is not
exponential:

    μ     λ₀(drop 3)
   38     +0.38
   62     +0.093
   74     +0.090
   80     +0.090

An ansatz
λ₀ = A e^{−c L}
fitted on 38→62 would
predict λ₀(80)≈0.03,
already three times
below the floor. The
linear model predicted
a *sign change*. The
exponential model
predicts a *further
drop*. Both miss the
plateau.

The full well can be
exponential because it
is a cancellation
A−P on a large space
(NB=80). The drop-3
remainder is a O(0.1)
shift of that
cancellation, and that
shift has stopped
moving. Exponentials
do not apply to a
stuck offset.
