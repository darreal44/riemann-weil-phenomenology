# Slepian pairs versus (v₀, v₁) of Q

K_nm = ∫_0^{γ₁} η̂_n(g) η̂_m(g) dg
in the hat basis. ψ₀, ψ₁ =
top two eigenvectors of K
(desert band [0, first zero]).
v₀, v₁ = well and partner of Q
on χ₅.

The pairing is visible
(diagonal dominates) and
not sharp (cross ~ 0.5).

## Shapes, μ=8

    v₀  ( 0.80, −0.60,  0.04, …)   even combo of φ₀,φ₁
    ψ₀  ( 0.96, −0.18, −0.17, …)   almost pure φ₀
    v₁  ( 0.57,  0.73, −0.29, …)   odd combo
    ψ₁  ( 0.00, −0.50,  0.80, …)   starts at φ₁

The desert projector picks
the flattest hat. Q picks
the even combination that
cancels A−P. Those are
different vectors in the
same 2-plane.

## Reading

(v₀, v₁) is a Slepian *pair*
in the weak sense: two
head-concentrated modes,
even/odd under the hat
shift, gap Δℓ ≈ 12.

It is not the Slepian pair
of the desert [0, γ₁]. The
desert explains that two
modes fit; P chooses which
combination is the well.
Replacing Q by K would
put the well on φ₀ and
miss the 10⁻⁶…10⁻¹⁷
scale (K's eigenvalues
here are O(1), un-normalized
energy, not a well).
