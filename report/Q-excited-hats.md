## Reading

N_eff counts head-concentrated
eigenvectors of Q, not
Slepians of the time window
alone. The well and one
partner stay in {φ₀,φ₁,φ₂}.
Everything past that is
already a tail mode, including
the “small” 10⁻³ and 10⁻⁴
that look like a well if
one only reads the size.
