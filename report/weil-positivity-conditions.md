# Conditions for Weil positivity

To claim Q≥0 in the sense that implies RH / GRH, all of the following
are required.

1. **Admissible
   class.** Even tests with decay on θ and ˆθ (`admissible-tests.md`).
   Finite cosine sums in W_L are in the class for Q_pk.

2. **Identified
   A.** The archimedean pairing must be A_∞, not a mixed CST+ψ writing
   (#71). Done on W_L.

3. **All
   lags.** Every n with log n < L (p^k included). That is Q_pk, not the
   old Q_window that dropped powers.

4. **All
   θ in the class, one L.** A take (S_lo>0) attempts this with a tail
   bound. A positive H_n does not (Courant from above).

5. **All
   L.** The missing quantifier. Takes died by μ=8.1. H_n stays + and
   sinks.

Drop any one of 1–5 and the implication to RH fails. The lab has 1–3 on
W_L for the quadratic χ that were coded, 4 only as dead or flickering
takes, 5 not at all.
