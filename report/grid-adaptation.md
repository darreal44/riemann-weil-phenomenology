# Grid adaptation

#87 rule: among well+rise slabs, bisect the one with largest leftover =
g''(left)−m_i. Eight times, starting from uniform N=4+4. Tail untouched
(spent).

That is h-adaptivity driven by the legal estimator, not by I_true−I_lo
and not by |G−K|. p-adaptivity (raising the degree on a slab) would be
Hermite or a cubic with a g^{(4)} bound — rejected as a lower bound.

Next legal moves: more bisections (diminishing returns,
`leftover-convergence.md`), or stop. The window on this v is already
met. Adapting the grid on another v or another μ is a new witness, not a
class argument.
