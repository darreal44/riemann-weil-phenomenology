Iwasawa
studies
modules
over
Z_p[[Γ]]
as

climbs
the
cyclotomic
Z_p-extension

number

(or
a
p-adic
Lie

The
main
conjecture
(now

Mazur–Wiles,
Kato,
… )

the
characteristic

of
a
Selmer

class

with
a
p-adic
L-function.

Objects:
μ- and
λ-invariants,
p-adic
L_p(s,χ),
Iwasawa
algebra,
not
W_L
and
not
Γ_R(s).

- “μ”

  this

  Here

  a
  lag
  log p^k

  explicit

  There
