lag, Q_{n,n+k} oscillates (std 0.20 on k=1). The closest object is B₁, a
modulated codiagonal, with a proved ‖B₁^Θ‖≤4|w₂|/π and an essential
lower bound that lives in the Hankel piece, not in B₁.

Szegő, Widom, Gohberg–Semencul need a symbol independent of n. They stop
here.
