# Node, fluctuation impact, topological insulator

## The node — already here, not the server

On the dumped modes, Φ(γ_a)·v0 and Φ(γ_b)·v0 have opposite signs
(χ₂₉ μ=22: +/− on 10.42–11.20; χ₁₇: −/+ on 15.64–16.27). That is
the node. Q and Gram agree at overlap 1.0. No assemble to rerun.

## Impact of the cloud

v^T G v = 2 Σ_γ amp(γ)^2 = λ0. The pair carries 61 % (χ₂₉) and 43 %
(χ₁₇) of that sum. Dropping the cloud rescales λ0 by the pair
fraction and shifts ell by −ln(mass_pair):

| | ell | pair mass | −ln(mass) | shift |
|---|-----|-----------|-----------|-------|
| χ₂₉ μ=22 | 5.62 | 61 % | 0.49 | 9 % of ell |
| χ₁₇ μ=22 | 12.64 | 43 % | 0.84 | 7 % of ell |

Quantum fluctuations (the 10–18 zero tail) change the depth by
≲ 1 nat. They do not make the singlet. The node does.

## Topological insulator

A TI needs a bulk gap in a Brillouin zone and a protected edge.
The Gram gap λ₁/λ₀ is large, but the index j is not momentum: there
is no k-space, no edge, no Chern. The window is a finite 1D
interval. The odd node is a point defect in frequency, not a
boundary state of a filled band.

SSH on the cosine chain would require alternating hoppings we do
not have. The survey found no second odd mode to play the role of
a counter-propagating edge. There is no topological insulator here.
