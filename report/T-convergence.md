# Convergence of T

Two different T.

## 1. Height T of a zero list

lfunzeros(L, T_req) returns T_last ≤ T_req. At T_req=320
we have T_last ∈ [318.9, 320). The missing slice is O(mean
gap) ~ 2π/log T ≈ 0.7. Not a bias.

Weyl n / N(T_last) → 1 from below after the −T/π term for
GL2. Measured 1.00 on the eight curves. That is the
convergence of the *count*, not of an eigenvalue.

Raising T_req from 80 to 320 added zeros; it did not
change γ1 and barely changed Gram ell at μ=22 (those
windows only see γ ≲ 70). T_req > 80 is for μ=38 Gram
and for Weyl, not for the singlet at μ=22.

## 2. Hecke T_p = a_p

Deligne: |a_p| ≤ 2√p. The ratio a_p/(2√p) ∈ [−1,1] and
follows Sato–Tate in the p→∞ limit. With twelve primes
we do not see that limit. T_p does not “converge” to a
number; the empirical measure of the angles does.

T_p is not a sequence in L. No rate in μ.
