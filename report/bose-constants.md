# Bose constants

The archimedean piece of A is ½ w(y) (2 e^{-(2s0−1) y} − θ_v(y)) up to
the lab’s normalisation. For Dirichlet L-functions s₀ ∈ {1/4, 3/4} (even
or odd character).

    even (χ₅, s₀=1/4): 2 e^{−3y/2}, w = 2 e^{−y/2} / (1−e^{−2y})
    odd  (χ₃, s₀=3/4): 2 e^{−y/2}, w = 2 e^{−3y/2} / (1−e^{−2y})

Those are the only two Bose factors in the Dirichlet plane. #90 swapped
them and the well survived. The constants are not free parameters: they
are fixed by Γ_R(s+s₀) in the completed Λ.

Changing s₀ off {1/4, 3/4} is another L (weight, Maass), not a third
Bose knob on the same χ.

Not a campaign.
