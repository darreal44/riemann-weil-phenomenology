## Reading

Prolates tell the dimension
of the head (N_eff ~ 2, not
γ₁ L/π). They do not tell
λ₀. To get the depth 14…39
from a prolate one would
need c ~ ℓ₀/2 ≈ 7…20 *and*
to keep only the ground
state, which is the desert
count we already rejected.
