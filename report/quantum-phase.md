# Quantum phase transitions — what is not there

A QPT needs a T=0 tuning parameter and a closing gap between two
ground states. μ is a bandwidth / type, not that parameter (the
isolation vs μ is a crossover, `phase-crossover.md`).

The candidate control is the charged-pair gap δ, at fixed μ=22 on
χ₂₉, other zeros frozen (synthetic Gram).

| δ/ν | ell₀ | λ₁/λ₀ |
|-----|------|-------|
| 0.05 | 7.13 | 1500 |
| 0.38 (real) | 5.64 | 336 |
| 1.00 | 5.06 | 180 |
| 2.00 | 7.28 | 55 |

Squeezing the pair deepens the singlet. Opening it toward Nyquist
shallows it, then another pair takes over (ell rises again, gap
ratio drops). λ₀ never closes against λ₁. No level crossing, no
critical δ.

Two-impurity Kondo (RKKY vs screening) would be two pairs with
comparable mass and λ₀ ∼ λ₁. The spectrum we have is one isolated
odd state. That is a crossover into a singlet phase, not a quantum
critical point.

A genuine QPT search would need a family where two tight pairs sit
at comparable window coupling — we have not seen one in the sixteen
lists. Until that spectrum appears, there is no quantum phase
transition to analyse.
