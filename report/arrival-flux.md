# Arrival flux versus tower sum

In L-time the prime
rate is e^L / L. Each
new p has eventual
payload |2w| ∼ 2 L e^{−L/2}.
The *eventual* mass
arriving per unit L is

    (e^L / L) · 2 L e^{−L/2}
        = 2 e^{L/2}

which is the derivative
of 2 e^{L/2} ∼ 2 √μ, the
same divergence as
∑_{p≤μ} log p / √p.

Nothing new: the walk
of `all-towers.md` is
this flux, delayed by
the drift y/L: 1→0.
At arrival the flux
contributes 0 to P.
The 2 e^{L/2} is what
P would gain if every
new prime sat at 2I
immediately — which it
does not.

A is fed by D₂ ∼ e^{−y/2}
on [0,L] and saturates.
The flux 2 e^{L/2} is
*not* a term in A. That
is the mismatch that
makes “A_∞ − P_∞” the
wrong picture.
