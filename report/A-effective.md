A is not a constant of the hole. It falls
through 1 at μ≈16, the same place extra
sub-Nyquist pieces appear and the union
λ_max saturates.

A derived sampling constant would have to
depend on K, or be stated only for the
one-set Λ_early, not for (γ₁,γ₂) alone.
