# α coefficients

    Q(v) = λ₀ α₀² + λ₁ α₁² + λ₂ α₂²
    α₀ = ⟨v, v_min⟩
    α₁² + α₂² = 1 − α₀²

We only have α₀
(the json). λ₀ is
2.3×10⁻⁶ at μ=16,
so λ₀ α₀² is
invisible next to
Q ~ 10⁻³. Almost
all of Q on the
pencil is bulk:

    v         α₀     1−α₀²     Q    Q/(1−α₀²)
    (5,−4,1) 0.9985  0.0029  0.0014   0.48
    (4,−3,1) 0.9944  0.0113  0.0055   0.49
    (1,−1,0) 0.9907  0.0185  0.0094   0.51
    (3,−2,1) 0.9806  0.0384  0.0190   0.50
    (4,−3,0) 0.9925  0.0150  0.0005   0.03

Four rays sit on
Q ≈ 0.50 (1−α₀²)
at μ=16: one
effective bulk
eigenvalue ~0.5.
(4,−3,0) does not:
same α₀, five
times less Q. Its
(α₁,α₂) sits on
the *smaller* bulk
mode, or cancels.
One coefficient
does not fix Q.

At μ=150 the
prefactor drifts
down (0.27–0.39)
and spreads. λ₀
is 10⁻⁸, still
irrelevant to Q(v).

To split α₁ vs α₂
one needs the other
two eigenvectors
of H. They were
not shipped. The
missing line is
those two vectors
at μ=16 and 150,
not another μ.
