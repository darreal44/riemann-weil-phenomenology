# Convergence of v₀

χ₅. Sign convention: first
large component of (v₀,v₁)
positive.

The direction freezes by N=8
to 10⁻⁴ in overlap. λ₀ keeps
dropping (more hats, deeper
well). The tail after φ₂ is
5×10⁻³ and stops growing:
v₀ lives in three hats.

| μ | λ₀ | v₀ | v₁ | v₂ | ⟨v, v(8)⟩ | arg(v₀,v₁) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 9.6×10⁻⁷ | 0.796 | −0.604 | 0.036 | 1.000 | −37.2° |
| 11 | 1.7×10⁻⁹ | 0.752 | −0.651 | 0.103 | 0.996 | −40.9° |

| 22 | 1.0×10⁻¹⁴ | 0.684 | −0.693 | 0.230 | 0.971 | −45.3° |

A slow rotation in the 2-plane
toward the diagonal (45°),
plus a growing φ₂ (0.04 → 0.23).
Overlap stays above 0.97 from
μ=8 to 22. That is why v₀(8)
remains a witness.

## Reading

N-convergence of the *shape*
is cheap; N-convergence of λ₀
is not. μ-convergence of the
shape is a drift of a few
degrees per octave of μ, not
a jump. A frozen rational
(4/5, −3/5, 1/5) tracks the
μ=16 vector to a few percent
and is a candidate for the
six-term certificate.
