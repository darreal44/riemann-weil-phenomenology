## Reading

H is SPD on these windows, with
one moderate eigenvalue and one
small-but-polynomial one, axis
locked on e₁. The exponential
well of Q is the Schur term
C T⁻¹ Cᵀ eating this H, not
H itself.

A bound λ_min(H) ≥ c μ^{−5}
on the 2-plane would be
compatible with the table and
still would not lower-bound
λ₀(Q).
