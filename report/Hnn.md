# Diagonal H_nn, χ₅ μ=16

    n     A_nn      P_nn      H_nn     P/A
    0    −1.383    −1.475     0.092    1.07
    1     0.820     0.627     0.194    0.76

    3     1.771    −1.242     3.013   −0.70

n=0: same sign, |P|≳|A|,
tiny positive remainder —
the well lives here.

n=1,2: same sign, |P|<|A|,
H_nn = O(0.2), the 3-plane
bulk.

n=3: opposite sign. P
flips and −P adds to A.
H₃₃ jumps to 3. That is
the first hat whose prime
sum does not track the
archimedean diagonal.

The head of Q is the
region where P tracks A
(n≤2). φ₃ is the first
hat that leaves that
region. Higher n will
look like n=3 or worse
(θ_nn oscillates more,
primes do not follow A).
