# Two Weyl laws

**Zeros.** N(T) ~ (T/2π) log(T/2π) for ζ, and the one-sided Weyl for
L(s,χ) used in harvest_weyl. That law is in the repo and was corrected
to one side (ratio ~1 at T=320). It counts γ, not eigenvalues of Q̂_L.

**Operator.** If Q̂_L were positive with a reasonable kernel, a Weyl law
would read #{n : λ_n ≤ λ} ~ c Area(phase space) or ~ c n^α for the
counting function of the cosine Gram. We do not have the bulk spectrum
(`Wlog3-spectrum.md`). H_n gives one number (the bottom) per n, not a
density of states.

The collapse of λ_min with L (10⁻⁵ at μ=5 to 10⁻¹⁰ at μ=8) is not a Weyl
law. It is the ground state sinking as the window grows. A Weyl law
would say how many eigenvalues sit above that floor, and that count is
not computed.

Do not confuse the two. Harvest Weyl = zeros. Spectral Weyl = not
available.
