Split:

    deep (ℓ>2)     χ₅: 3     χ₁₃: 1
    plunge (½,2]   χ₅: 1     χ₁₃: 1
    anti (ℓ<0)     χ₅: 1     χ₁₃: 1

ℓ<0 means λ(G)>1 on that
kernel direction: out-of-cut
mass larger than 1. Those
vectors vanish on the
in-band zeros below ω* and
still carry O(1) mass on
the later zeros. They are
in ker Eval and *not* in
the well.

The well-count at cut 2
is the deep block. K* is
deep + plunge + anti.
The two extra dimensions
are real, they just are
not deep. A bound
‖G|ker‖≤e^{−2} would have
forced them into the deep
block; the spectrum says
they refuse.
