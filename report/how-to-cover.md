# How the covering step could be taken

Not a plan. The step is

    inf_{f ∈ W_L} Q_L(f) ≥ 0
    for every L > 0.

W_L = Paley–Wiener of
type L (or Weil’s class
of compactly supported
even test functions,
or a dense subclass
that still sees every
off-line zero).

## Routes that would count

1. A minorant of Q_L
   that does not go
   through a finite
   basis. Beurling /
   Landau–Widom on the
   *union* desert +
   first zeros, as one
   set — the item the
   repo already marked
   open. A constant
   times |E| is not
   enough; the sign of
   Q−(that minorant)
   has to stay plus.

2. An identity
   Q_L(f)=‖T_L f‖²
   with T_L built from
   primes and the
   archimedean place
   only (SOS / Cholesky
   of the form, not of
   a matrix). The
   numerical Cholesky
   at μ=11 is not that.

3. Li coefficients
   λ_n≥0 for all n.
   Equivalent to RH.
   A proof of λ_n≥0
   is the covering
   step in another
   basis. The repo
   does not compute
   them as a criterion.

4. A spectral gap of
   an operator whose
   positivity is
   unconditional and
   whose kernel sees
   the off-line zeros
   (Connes, de Branges).
   Identification
   d*λ ↔ d*u is not
   that gap.

## Why the current objects
do not take it

Courant on V_N gives
λ_min(V_N) ≥ inf Q.
Wrong direction
(#42). Adding hats
makes the upper bound
on the inf *worse*
(deeper well), not
better.

A witness v with
Q(v)>0 is one ray.
(∀ L) on one ray is
already open; on the
class it is another
quantifier.

Schur Δ>0 on a 3×3
is Q>0 on the hat
space *after* T>0.
The hat space is not
W_L.

## What a real attempt
has to produce

A lower bound for
Q_L(f) that is
- uniform in f∈W_L
  (or a dense set),
- explicit in L,
- non-negative for
  all L,
- and that uses only
  primes + Gamma,
  not the zeros.

Until that bound
exists, the list can
be finished and RH
stays on the other
side of the covering
implication.
