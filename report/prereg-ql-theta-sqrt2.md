# Preregistration: ‖Θ(y)‖ ≤ √2 for L/4 ≤ y < L/2

Locked before the run. Geometric cap
on W_L when the #59 lemma (y ≥ L/2)
does not apply. Then Neumann Q_pk,
χ₃, μ=5, with that cap on log 2.
Finite sections of Θ are Courant the
wrong way for a cap; they must not
exceed √2. Not Galerkin of Q̂_L.
Not RH.

**Lemma.** Even L² on [−L/2, L/2],
θ(f)(y)=2⟨f, T_y f⟩, hat-unit f.
If L/4 ≤ y < L/2, the half-window
[0, L/2] splits into four L² pieces
A, M₁, M₂, C with A,C the shift
pair (disjoint) and M₂ = reverse(A)
on [0, y]. Then

    |θ| ≤ 2√(αβ) + 2√(α μ₂) + μ₁ ≤ √2

where α,β,μ₁,μ₂ are the masses.
At μ=5, y=log 2 sits in the band
(log 4 < 5 < 16). log 3 and log 4
stay ≥ L/2, cap 1 as in #59.

and ‖Θ_64‖ > 1.41 (sharp). t_atoms
