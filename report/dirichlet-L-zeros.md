# Zeros of Dirichlet L-functions

L(s,χ) for primitive χ has trivial zeros from Γ((s+a)/2) and non-trivial
zeros in the critical strip. GRH: those have Re=1/2. The first γ grows
roughly like the desert ~ 2π / log(q) in the mean, larger for small q
odd characters sometimes (χ₃ has a late γ₁ relative to ζ).

This repo harvested γ to T=320 for many χ (`harvest_weyl`, one-sided
Weyl ratio ~1) and used them in Gram / MUSIC, not in Q_pk on W_log3 (the
zero sum is empty there).

Q≥0 on all admissible θ is GRH for that χ (Weil). A take at one L is a
tiny zero-free statement in the range ˆθ sees. Dead takes do not move
any γ.

No new moisson is required to discuss the zeros: the pkl files already
list them. The open problem is not a missing γ; it is (∀ L) Q_L≥0 or a
negative identified section.
