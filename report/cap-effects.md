# Effects of the ‖Θ‖ cap

Two caps in the tail bound:

    y ≥ L/2     ‖Θ‖ ≤ 1 (or 2 in older scripts)
    L/4 ≤ y < L/2 ‖Θ‖ ≤ √2 (#72, finite sections saturate)

t_atoms = ∑ |w_n| ‖Θ(log n)‖. Lowering the cap lowers t_atoms and can
create a take that π and the lags would otherwise kill.

At μ=5, log 2 is in the √2 band. #72: t_atoms 1.327 → 1.040 when the cap
drops from 2 to √2. S_lo(16) becomes +5×10⁻⁶. The W_log5 take for χ₃
*is* a cap effect plus p^k plus identified A.

At μ=5.1 T₅ enters the same band (log 5 ≈ L near the edge, then drifts).
Cap √2 still leaves t_atoms=1.76 and ρ=1.024. No take. A still lower cap
would be a different lemma, not the measured saturation at √2.

The cap does not change H_n (H_n uses θ_hat, not the operator norm). It
only changes S_lo. That is why H₁₆ stays + while the take dies: two
objects.

A proof of ‖Θ‖≤√2 on L/4≤y<L/2 is still listed as finite-section
evidence, not a closed lemma (#72). If that lemma fails and the cap
returns to 2, the μ=5 χ₃ take dies retroactively.
