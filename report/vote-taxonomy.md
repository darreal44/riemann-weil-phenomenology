# Taxonomy of votes

A vote is the sign
of λ₀(Q_{−p}).
Labels are pairs
(p, μ), not
permanent names.

    mute          p not in the sum,
                  or drop-p = full
    dispensable   drop-p > 0
    necessary     drop-p < 0

Modifiers:

    edge          y/L ≥ 0.95
    interior      y/L < 0.95
    heavy         |a_p| / 2√p ≳ 0.8
    delayed       necessary only
                  after a positive
                  stay past arrival

37a1, judged:

    p     μ=84           μ=86         μ=100
    3     dispensable    necessary    necessary
          interior       interior     interior
    83    mute-as-drop   (not dropped (necessary
          dispensable     at 86)       + edge)
          + edge
    89    absent         absent       dispensable
                                      + edge
    97    absent         absent       dispensable
                                      + almost mute

Delayed applies to
83’s own drop
(84⁺ → 100), not
to the dent on 3
(two units). 3 is
not delayed: it
was optional on a
long plateau then
necessary; it did
not arrive at 84.

A vote can be
edge and
necessary (83 at
100). A vote can
be interior and
dispensable (3 at
84). The four
cells
edge/interior ×
dispensable/necessary
are all occupied
on this curve.
