# Implications of “delayed”

If delayed ⇔
dispensable at some
μ₀>p and necessary
later, then 3 is
delayed (plateau
62–84, flip (84,86])
and 83 is delayed
(arrival 84, flip
by 100). The word
does not separate
them.

What we meant in
the journal was
narrower:

    delayed-on-arrival:
    dispensable at p⁺
    and necessary later.

#56: drop-83 is
already necessary
at 86. delayed-on-
arrival for 83 is
Δμ ∈ (0,2], same
bin as 3 after the
dent. 3 arrived at
3. 89 and 97 are
dispensable at 100.


Implications:

1. Do not predict
   the next flip
   from “heavy +
   edge at arrival”.
   83 was that and
   did not flip
   drop-83 at p⁺.
   It flipped 3
   two units later.

2. Delay is not
   a time-to-vote
   formula.
   Δμ(83 own vote)
   ∈ (0,2].
   Δμ(3 after the
   dent) ∈ (0,2].
   Same bin (#56).

3. A delayed vote
   does not weaken
   full Q. Both
   delayed primes
   leave 37a1 PSD
   at 250.

4. Transfer to ζ
   would need a
   drop-p series
   for a_n=1, which
   we do not have.
   The implication
   “expect delayed
   votes on ζ” is
   a guess, not a
   theorem.
