# Implications of “delayed”

If delayed ⇔
dispensable at some
μ₀>p and necessary
later, then 3 is
delayed (plateau
62–84, flip (84,86])
and 83 is delayed
(arrival 84, flip
by 100). The word
does not separate
them.

What we meant in
the journal was
narrower:

    delayed-on-arrival:
    dispensable at p⁺
    and necessary later.

Only 83 has that
(among the primes
we dropped). 3
arrived at 3, long
before the plateau.
89 and 97 are
dispensable at
their first
measured point
(100); they may
join the same
class later.

Implications:

1. Do not predict
   the next flip
   from “heavy +
   edge at arrival”.
   83 was that and
   did not flip
   drop-83 at p⁺.
   It flipped 3
   two units later.

2. Delay is not
   a time-to-vote
   formula.
   Δμ(83 own vote)
   ∈ (16,16+] from
   84 to 100, not
   pinned. Δμ(3
   after the dent)
   ∈ (0,2].

3. A delayed vote
   does not weaken
   full Q. Both
   delayed primes
   leave 37a1 PSD
   at 250.

4. Transfer to ζ
   would need a
   drop-p series
   for a_n=1, which
   we do not have.
   The implication
   “expect delayed
   votes on ζ” is
   a guess, not a
   theorem.
