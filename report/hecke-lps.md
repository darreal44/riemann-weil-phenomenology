# Hecke operators on LPS graphs

On the LPS Cayley graph, the adjacency at norm p *is* the Hecke operator
T_p acting on functions on the class set (or on PGL₂(F_q)). The spectrum
of that graph is the spectrum of T_p on the corresponding automorphic
space. Ramanujan–Petersson is |a_p| ≤ 2√p, which is exactly the
Sato–Tate bound already tabulated for 11a1 in this repo
(`sato_tate.py`).

That does not make T_p act on the window of Q̂. Q sees a_n as Weil
coefficients when we scan GL₂, not as a Cayley generator of an expander.

Not a new harvest.
