# Enclosure transfer: χ₈ μ=16, same integral

χ₈ is even (a=0), so s₀=1/4
and the integrand a(y) is
*identical* to χ₅ at the
same μ. Only CST and P
change.

    CST₈ = log(8/π) − γ − log(1−1/256)
         = +0.361410

    ∫₀ᴸ a  = A₅ − CST₅
           ≈ −0.71915
           (from the χ₅ ball
           [−0.82939, −0.82692]
           minus CST₅=−0.10859
           ⇒ [−0.72080, −0.71833])

    A₈ = CST₈ + ∫ a
       ∈ [−0.35939, −0.35692]

## P₈, ramified 2 dropped

χ₈ vanishes on even n.
Terms n=3,5,7,9,11,13
(same θ_v, new χ):

    P₈ = −0.444678

    Q₈ = A₈ − P₈
       ∈ [0.08529, 0.08776]

Measured Rayleigh 0.0868
sits inside. Positive by
a centimetre, not a
millimetre (χ₅ was 0.004).
The conductor ate the
powers of 2, which were
the dangerous negative
terms for χ₅.

## Status

This is a transfer of the
χ₅ integral ball, not a
new quadrature. It uses
θ_v already tabulated and
an elementary CST. Judge
not shipped: one function
of `av_enclose` plus the
χ₈ table above would do
it. Odd characters (χ₃, χ₄)
have a=1, s₀=3/4, a
*different* integrand —
not this transfer.
