# Rate of λ_min(S_N) → c_L^*

None measured.
None claimed.

If Q̂_L were
an integral
operator with
smooth kernel
on a 1-d
interval, the
compression
error on the
first N
cosines would
typically be
O(N^{-k}) for
some k set by
the smoothness
of G, or
exponential
if G is
analytic on a
strip. D₂ is
analytic on
(0,L] and
singular at 0
like 1/y.
After
regularisation
G_reg is
smooth on
[−L,L]. A
polynomial
rate is
plausible.
Not proved.

What the
χ₄ table
shows is not
that rate.
λ_H(h) is
λ_min of the
*head*, with
the tail
still split
off. It
drops 0.084
→ 0.070 from
h=2 to 20
(about
0.0007 per
mode) and
then moves
0.0001 from
20 to 24.
That is the
head
settling,
not
λ_min(S_N)
settling
onto c_L^*.

β(h) rising
0.004 →
0.008 from
h=20 to 24
is the
Schur
majorant
improving
(more of Q
in H, less
in C), also
not a rate
for c_L^*.

To see a
rate one
would need
S_N for
N=4,8,16,32
on the same
form, same
χ, and a
fit. That
is Galerkin,
and it still
would not
prove the
sign. Not
scheduled.
