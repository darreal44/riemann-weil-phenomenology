## Three saturations, not one

1. **C saturates first.** χ₂₉ is flat from μ=11. χ₅ is flat on 38–62.
   χ₁₇ drops once (0.21→0.11) then freezes. The efficiency of E is
   decided by the low-lying shape of Γ.

2. **s_loc saturates slower.** Δs_loc per twelve units of μ falls
   0.14 → 0.01. χ₅ saturates from *above* (2.28→2.04). The linear
   law ell ≈ s μ + b is an intermediate window, not an asymptote.

3. **The spectral gap explodes.** λ₁/λ₀ : 6 → 10⁶. Isolation of v₀
   is nonlinear in μ: each extra type makes the ground state
   exponentially cheaper than the first harmonic. Higher modes stay
   O(1) (previous note). Saturation of C is this isolation — once
   only one mode hides, extra pieces of E cannot be assigned.

The nonlinearity is the product of a shrinking Nyquist step with a
fixed low-lying pair. Not a cubic term in |E|.
