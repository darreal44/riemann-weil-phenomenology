# Measure of M₂

M₂=[2y−L/2, y]. Length L/2−y = |A| = |C|. ρ(x)=y−x sends A=[0,L/2−y]
onto M₂: ρ(0)=y, ρ(L/2−y)=2y−L/2. Same length, not the same mass unless
|h| is even under ρ across those two pieces.

At the take, each is ~14 % of the half-window by length. The simplex
vertex wants μ₂=1/4 and α=1/2, so twice as much mass on A as on M₂
despite equal length: h must be larger on A than on M₂.

CS on AM₂ is 2√(αμ₂). Equal length does not force α=μ₂.

Not a new cap.
