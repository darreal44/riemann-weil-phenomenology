# Krein on the cutoff kernel

Cutoff: G(y)=0 for
|y|>L. That *is*
an extension of
G|_{[-L,L]} to ℝ.
#57: its symbol
m_Q dips below 0,
so this extension
is not PD. Krein
is silent on it.

Krein on the
*restriction*:
is G|_{[-L,L]}
PD as a kernel
on delays ≤L?
If yes, some
other extension
is PD, and the
cutoff just
wasn’t it.
If no, no
extension is PD
and Q̂_L ≱ 0.

That restriction
test is not
Bochner on the
cutoff. It is
the interval
form
(`krein-proof.md`).
Hats and cosines
are samples.
#58 is a
different
sufficient
bound (Schur
tail in the
cosine ONB) and
takes χ₈
without
producing a
PD extension
of the cutoff.

So: coming back
to Krein on the
cutoff does not
mean re-testing
m_Q. It means
testing PD of
the restricted
kernel — full G,
g(0)=0, atom
included — on
[−L,L]. χ₈ is
the case where
a proof is
closest (#58
already took
the class by
another door;
a Krein μ
would be a
second
certificate,
now with w₂=0
and a 5-atom
picture of
G_reg).
