# Pointwise Fourier convergence

On the interval, the cosine series of an even H¹ function converges
pointwise everywhere it is continuous (Dirichlet–Jordan) and almost
everywhere in L² (Carleson). The hats are already the basis vectors, so
there is nothing to sum for φ_n itself.

Partial sums of θ_v = ∑ v_n v_m θ_nm are finite (the 3-plane is N=3).
Pointwise convergence is not an issue for g or for #89.

Gibbs appears if one extends the kernel by zero past L and then sums a
Fourier series on a larger period. That is Bochner’s cutoff again, not a
new phenomenon.
