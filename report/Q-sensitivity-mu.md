# Sensitivity of Q, μ=16 → 150

Same five pencil
rays, χ₅, 3-plane
(`av-other-v-mu150.json`).

    v            Q16      Q150    Q150/Q16
    (4,−3,1)   0.00551   0.00410    0.74
    (5,−4,1)   0.00139   0.00103    0.74
    (4,−3,0)   0.00051   0.00029    0.57
    (1,−1,0)   0.00942   0.00702    0.75
    (3,−2,1)   0.01900   0.01415    0.74
    e₀         0.092     0.074      0.80
    e₁         0.194     0.152      0.78
    e₂         0.239     0.169      0.71

Four of five pencil
rays scale by **0.74**.
(4,−3,0) drops more
(0.57) — it is the
closest to v_min
(Q=5×10^{-4} already
at 16). λ_min itself
falls 2.3×10⁻⁶ →
7.8×10⁻⁹ (factor
3×10^{-3}), much
faster than Q(v) on
these rays.

So Q on a fixed
rational ray is
weakly sensitive to
μ on this range
(−25% for ×9 in μ).
The ground-state
value is extremely
sensitive (the well
deepens). Those are
different
sensitivities
(`v-sensitivity.md`
was ∇_v at fixed μ).

Sign is stable on
the pencil. The
fragile object is
λ_min, not Q(v).
