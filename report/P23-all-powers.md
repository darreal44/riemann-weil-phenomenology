# All powers of 2 and 3, not just 2 and 3

The −2.25 limit was
2w₂+2w₃, n=2 and n=3
only. Every p^k has
Θ→2I, so the honest
2–3 tower is

    ∑_{k≥1} 2 w(p^k)
    w(p^k)= χ(p)^k log p / p^{k/2}

Geometric:

    2-tower → 2 log 2 · r₂/(1−r₂)
              r₂=−1/√2
            ≈ −0.575
    3-tower → 2 log 3 · r₃/(1−r₃)
              r₃=−1/√3
            ≈ −0.821
    both    → ≈ −1.40

Alternating powers
(+4, −8, +16, +9, −27…)
eat more than half of
the −2.25 scare.

At μ=150 those extra
powers are only
partially in
(4,8,9,16,27,32,64,81).
P_rest is negative
(−0.024) in part
because +9 and +16
have not reached their
positive 2I values
while −8, −27 have
started.

The unfinished limit
of the 2–3 *towers*
is −1.40, next to
A(150)=−1.64, not
−2.25 next to −1.64.
The gap is a different
size than
`P3-limit.md` said.
