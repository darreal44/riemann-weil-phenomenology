# L²[0, L] does not kill the anti block

The cosine hats are an
ONB of L²[0, L]:

    η₀ = L^{−1/2},
    η_n = (2/L)^{1/2} cos(2π n t / L).

So ‖η_x‖_{L²[0,L]} = ‖x‖₂.
The sphere already used
in G is the function-space
sphere. Switching the
label does not move spec(G).

What can exceed 1 is the
*sample* energy 2∑|η_x(γ)|²,
because Φ (evaluate at the
zeros) is not Plancherel.
Anti-wells are a property
of the point set Γ, not of
a wrong time-norm.

A norm that would fold
them back is a frequency
L² norm on {γ ≥ ω*} — i.e.
treat the later zeros as a
measure and ask for the
operator norm of analysis
on that measure, restricted
to ker Eval_ω*. That is
again ‖G|_ker‖, which we
already know is 3.4.

No free change of metric
removes the anti direction
without also rescaling the
deep wells by the same
Φ. The cut ℓ>2 stays a
cut in that metric.
