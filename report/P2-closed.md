# P₂(L) is closed

n=2, χ₅(2)=−1,
w₂ = −log 2 / √2 ≈ −0.490.

    P₂(L) = w₂ · vᵀ Θ(log 2, L) v

Θ_nm(y,L) is elementary
(sines, `th_hat`). At
y=log 2:

    μ=16  y/L=1/4   vᵀΘv = 1.122   P₂ = −0.550
    μ=50  y/L=0.177 vᵀΘv = 1.474   P₂ = −0.722

θ₀₀ = 2(1−y/L) alone
already goes 1.50 → 1.65.
The growth of |P₂| is
mostly that plus the
(0,1) block of Θ, not a
new prime.

Same for P₃ with
w₃ = −log 3 / √3 ≈ −0.634
and y=log 3.

The tracking A vs P₂+P₃
is then an explicit
function of one real
variable L: A is an
integral against D₂ on
[0,L], P₂+P₃ is two
evaluations of Θ.
That is the (∀ μ)
problem reduced to two
terms and an integral,
for this v and this χ.
Still not estimated.
