# Weight of T₅

    |w₅| = log 5 / √5 = 0.720 |w₃| = 0.634 |w₂| = 0.490

Heavier than T₃. Enters at μ>5, L=log 5. log 5 ≥ L/2 again (5≥√5), so
‖Θ(log 5)‖≤1 and ‖T₅‖≤0.720 when χ(5)≠0.

χ₅: χ(5)=0, T₅ absent on that character (same pattern as χ₃ and T₃). The
first window that sees T₅ on χ₅ is none; on χ₃,χ₄,χ₇,χ₈,χ₁₇ it is μ=5⁺.

The sequence |w_p|=log p/√p peaks near p=7 (0.735) then slowly falls
(p=11: 0.723). New primes do not get arbitrarily heavy. They do get more
numerous. The danger at larger L is count, not the next |w_p|.
