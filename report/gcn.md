# Graph convolutional networks

Kipf–Welling GCN is a first-order Chebyshev filter on a normalised
adjacency. Same as `gnn.md`: no graph, no labels, no certificate that a
layer is below g or that a spectrum is Θ.

The cosine ONB already *is* the Fourier basis of the path. A GCN on that
path would be a learned polynomial in the Neumann Laplacian, weaker than
writing the cosines down.

Not a campaign.
