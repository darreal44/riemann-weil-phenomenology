matrix
[κ_{n+m}]

Hankel.

(up

½

ℓ²-norm

π.

the
distance
from
the
symbol

the
Hilbert
symbol
that
distance
is
π
and
does
not
fall
when
the
leading
rows
are
deleted.

(already
in
the
repo)
describes
the
s-numbers
of
a
Hankel.

operator
norm
is
s_1=π.
Smaller
s_n
do
not
replace
s_1
in
Off_far.

So
“analyser
la
norme
de
Hankel”
on
the
tail
is
the
same
sentence
as
`hilbert-bound.md`.

new
number.

a
