# Preregistration: three coverings of #72

Locked before the run. The simplex
2√(αβ)+2√(αμ₂)+μ₁ ≤ √2 is already
a theorem (`theta-sqrt2-l2.md`).
What is still geometry is the
identification

    |θ| ≤ 2√(αβ)+2√(αμ₂)+μ₁

as the overlap count on the
half-window. Without that count
the χ₃ μ=5 take is a cap effect
read backwards. Not Weil. Not RH.

Even f on [−L/2, L/2],
θ=2⟨f, T_y f⟩, h=√2 f|_{[0,L/2]}.
For L/3 ≤ y < L/2 the integrand
support [y−L/2, L/2] splits into
five x-intervals, three covering
types:

  AC  : A ↔ C by the shift y
        (two copies, + and −)
  AM2 : A ↔ M₂ by the even fold
        (two copies)
  M1  : M₁ against itself

A=[0, L/2−y], C=[y, L/2],
M₂=reverse(A)=[2y−L/2, y],
M₁=[L/2−y, 2y−L/2]. Disjoint,
union [0, L/2]. Cauchy–Schwarz
on each type gives the majorant.
At μ=5, y=log 2 sits in (L/3, L/2).
L/4 ≤ y < L/3 is degeneration
(M₁ empty, reverse(A) meets A);
the take does not use it.

**Prediction.** The five intervals
partition the integrand support
on a grid in [L/3, L/2). Pair
integrals sum to ⟨f, T_y f⟩ for
the constant mode and a cosine.
|θ| ≤ majorant from the masses.
log 2 / log 5 ∈ (1/3, 1/2).

**Kill.** A gap in the partition,
or pair sum ≠ ⟨f, T_y f⟩, or
|θ| > majorant + 10⁻⁹ on those
tests, or log 2 / log 5 ≤ 1/3.
