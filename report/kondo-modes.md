# Kondo reading of the hiding mode (analogy, 5 September 2026)

Not a condensed-matter claim. A dictionary for the isolated v0.

## Dictionary

| Kondo | here |
|-------|------|
| Impurity (degenerate two-level) | the charged tight pair in Γ |
| Conduction band | cosine window of type τ = L/2 |
| Hybridisation V | the Fourier matrix Φ_n(γ) of the pair |
| Kondo singlet | v0, N_eff ≈ 1.7 |
| T_K | λ0 itself (or e^{-ell}) |
| Screening cloud | the window of length L |
| Charge-Kondo / single channel | one pair, one slow mode |
| Underscreened / extra channels | would be λ1 ≪ 1 with another pair |

The Kondo scale is exponential in 1/ρJ. Here λ0 is exponential in
L times an effective hole. The shape matches; the microscopic J is
the pairing of the window ground state with that pair, not an
exchange constant we measured.

## What the numbers say

χ₂₉ μ=22: λ0 = 4.2×10^{-3}, λ1/λ0 = 292, one pair at 10.42–11.20
(61 %). Single channel, screened.
χ₂₉ μ=11: λ1/λ0 = 6. The impurity is not yet locked into one
singlet — the “high temperature” side of the same crossover.
χ₁₇ μ=22: λ1/λ0 = 5×10^4. Deeper into the screened phase.
χ₅ μ=38: λ1/λ0 = 6×10^7. Same phase, larger T_K^{-1}.

Higher Gram modes stay O(1) and sit on *other* pairs: they are
band states, not extra Kondo channels. Saturation of C is complete
screening: once the singlet exists, further pieces of E do not
open a second channel.

## What would break the reading

A second eigenvalue with λ1 ~ λ0 and a second pair carrying mass
on v1. That would be two-channel / underscreened. We have not seen
it above μ=22 on the hold-outs. χ₂₉ at μ=11 is the only nearby
point, and it is the crossover, not a second channel.

## What it does not give

A formula for C(χ). In Kondo, T_K = D exp(-1/ρJ) still needs ρJ.
Here that is the coupling of the L-mode to the pair, which is the
object we already have and have not named beyond C. The analogy
rephrases isolation and saturation. It does not replace Beurling.
