# Impact of the diagonal H_nn

On v = (4, −3, 1)/√26,
χ₅ μ=16, 3-plane:

    Q_diag = ∑ v_n² H_nn = 0.133
             (0.057 + 0.067 + 0.009)
    Q_off  = −0.127
    Q(v)   =  0.0055

The well is not a small
diagonal. It is a 0.13
versus 0.13 cancellation
between diag and
off-diag. Killing any
one H_nn of the head
(0 or 1) would flip Q.

Gershgorin on the 3-plane
does not see this: every
disk of n=0,1,2 crosses
0 (left edge −0.19, −0.15,
−0.12). The cut ℓ>0 is
invisible to row sums.

H₃₃ is the opposite
regime:

    disk n=3: centre 3.01,
    radius 0.23, left 2.78

Isolated from 0. Setting
H₃₃=0 in the 4-plane
produces a negative
eigenvalue −0.074 — the
bulk mode falls through
and contaminates the
head. So H₃₃ *protects*
the 3-plane from φ₃; it
does not build the well.

Impact, in one line: H₀₀
and H₁₁ are the fuel of
Q_diag, H₃₃ is a wall
behind the head, and
neither Gershgorin nor
the size of H_nn reads
λ_min.
