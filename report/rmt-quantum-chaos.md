# Quantum chaos and RMT

Bohigas– Giannoni– Schmit: spectra of chaotic quantum systems look like
random matrices (GOE/GUE/GSE by time-reversal). Integrable systems look
like Poisson.

Montgomery– Odlyzko: zeros of ζ look like GUE. That is the empirical
link to a chaotic Hilbert–Pólya operator without time-reversal (GUE not
GOE).

This repo has three spectra that must not be mixed:

- γ
  of L(s,χ) — GUE candidate (not histogrammed here as the running
  branch).
- a_p
  of 11a1 — Sato–Tate (not GUE).
- eig(H_n)
  of Q̂_L — one ground state plus an O(1) bulk, not a GUE unfolding.

RMT does not prove RH and does not bound S_lo. A Marchenko–Pastur
comparison for H_n was already named as not fitting a Wishart null.
