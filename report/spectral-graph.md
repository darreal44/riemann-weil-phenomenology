# Spectral graph theory

Eigenvalues of a graph Laplacian or adjacency matrix control expansion,
cuts, random walks. The lab has no graph. The cosine ONB is the spectrum
of −d²/dx² with Neumann conditions on an interval, which is a continuous
limit of a path graph and is already used as an ONB, not as an expander.

Cheeger, Alon–Milman, or a graph Fourier transform do not bound θ(y) or
I_{[0,1]}. H_n is not the Laplacian of a Cayley graph on the primes.
