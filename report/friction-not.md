# “Friction” on A(L) — analogy only

There is no force and no
dissipation in Q. The
integrand of A carries
D₂(y) ∼ e^{−y/2}/(1−e^{−2y}).
Extending L adds a
thinner tail. That looks
like drag:

    dA₀₀/dL  is O(1) at
    small L and falls
    once most of the mass
    of D₂ is already in.

Measured slopes of A(v):

    Δμ      ΔA / ΔL
    16→29    −0.44
    29→37    −0.39
    37→50    −0.37

|dA/dL| shrinks slowly.
A linear drag
dA/dL = −γ (A−A_∞)
would be exponential
approach to a limit.
Four points do not
fix γ, and P is not a
smooth companion: it
jumps when a prime
crosses μ (then θ(L)=0
at the instant of
crossing, so the kick
is delayed into later L).

A Langevin cartoon
“A dragged, P kicked”
does not give the sign
of A−P. It redraws
`A00-L.md`. The repo
already closed Hubbard,
Kondo, BCS the same way.
