# Why 3 joins on (80, 100]

Not mostly θ(log 3).

    μ     L      log3/L
   80    4.382    0.251
  100    4.605    0.239

y/L of 3 moves 5%.
Θ toward 2I is a
slow drift, the same
one that sat at
+0.09 from 62 to 80.

Control at 74 and 80
with 71 (a=9) and 73
(a=−1) in the sum:
full well deepens
(ℓ 17.37→18.62),
drop-3 stays +0.090.
The plateau was not
a missing-prime
artifact (`gl2-37a1-
drop3-hi.md`).

The cliff is the
*next* arrivals:

    p     a_p    a_p / 2√p
   83    −15      0.82
   89      4      0.21
   97      4      0.20

83 is a heavy
interior prime on
(80,100]. Drop-3
at μ=100 is −0.42;
it then shelves
near −0.83 on
150–180 while the
full well keeps
deepening (ℓ 22→44).

## Mechanism

Without 3 the form
was a shallow well
(+0.09). Full λ₀
was 10^{-8}..10^{-9}:
3 was already
doing most of the
deepening, but the
remainder stayed
positive. New
heavy terms (83
first) rotate the
ground state and
push that remainder
through 0. With 3
kept, Q stays PSD.

So 3 “joins”
because the rest of
P without 3 is no
longer a witness,
not because
θ(log 3) suddenly
turns on. The
window that
matters is the
arrival of 83, not
the 5% of y/L.

Not isolated:
this run did not
drop 83 alone, nor
scan μ=82 vs 84.
That cut would
confirm the cliff
is 83 and not
89+97.
