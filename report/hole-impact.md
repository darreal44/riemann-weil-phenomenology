# Impact of the holes

Two kinds of holes in E_L: the desert (0,γ₁), compact, and the
long gaps Δ>ν, whose number grows.

## Share of |E|

| χ | μ | desert | # long | sum of long | desert/|E| |
|---|---|--------|--------|-------------|------------|
| χ₅ | 11 | 6.65 | 6 | 18.7 | 0.26 |
| χ₅ | 22 | 6.65 | 23 | 57.6 | 0.10 |
| χ₅ | 38 | 6.65 | 53 | 113 | 0.056 |
| χ₁₇ | 38 | 3.73 | 28 | 57 | 0.061 |
| χ₂₉ | 38 | 1.79 | 18 | 37 | 0.046 |

At the windows where s is measured, the desert is 5–10 % of |E|.
The extensive piece is the long gaps. A compact-defect correction
to D⁻ changes the *sampling certificate* at low μ; it changes
C(χ) L|E| by a few percent only.

## Impact on D⁻

Removing (0,γ₁) lifts D⁻ by 0.08 (χ₅), 0.06 (χ₁₇), 0.02 (χ₂₉)
(`compact-defects.md`). That is the whole compact effect on
density. Further cuts at 20 and 50 mix compact and non-compact
gaps and are not a hole-impact, they are a change of T.

## Impact on ell

ell ~ C L |E| = C L (γ₁ + Σ(Δ−ν)_+ + ν N_long), depending on
how one writes E. The desert term C L γ₁ at μ=38, C≈0.2, L≈3.6,
γ₁=6.65 is ~5 nats against ell≈87 (χ₅): about 6 %. The holes
that bite are the many long gaps, each just above ν, not the
one desert.

## Impact on the mode

v0 does not sit on the desert. The charged pair is a *short*
gap (under Nyquist), which is in S = complement of E, not a
hole of E. Holes of E are where the mode is *allowed* to hide;
the pair is where it *pays*. Impact of holes on v0 is indirect:
they set the type budget, they do not hold the mass.

## One line

Compact hole: D⁻. Growing holes: |E| and ell. Short gaps: the
singlet. Three impacts, three places.
