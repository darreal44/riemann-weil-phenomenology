# Gibbs

Partial sums of a jump overshoot by ≈9 % of the jump (the
Wilbraham–Gibbs constant). Here the jump is the zero extension of the
kernel past L. That overshoot is why the hard cutoff fails Bochner and
why a 2-adic grid at finite Λ walked through 0.49 and 0.71
(`2adic-numeric-lock.md`).

#89 and S_k do not sum a discontinuous periodic extension. Gibbs is not
their error term.
