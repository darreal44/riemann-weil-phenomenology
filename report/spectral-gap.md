# Spectral gap

gap := λ1/λ0 of the zero-side Gram (or of Q when Gram is
indefinite). Isolated pair iff gap ≫ 1 and N_eff ~ 2.

## Measured

| object | γ1 | gap μ=22 | gap μ=38 |
|--------|----|----------|----------|
| 11a1 | 6.36 | 1.2e5 | 6e5 |
| χ₂₉ | ~3 | 3e2 | 2e4 |
| Δ⊗χ₄ | 2.30 | 2e2 | 2e4 |
| Sym² E₁₁ | 3.90 | 6e2 | 3e3 |
| E₁₁⊗χ₅ | 2.35 | 40 | 1e2 |
| Δ⊗χ₅ | 0.89 | 2.2 | 5 |
| χ₃₁ Q | short | 26 | 3e3 |
| Δ, ζ Gram | 9.22 / 14.13 | INDEF | INDEF |

Gap grows with μ when there is a desert. It stays O(1)
when γ1 ≲ 1 (the window is already full of zeros).
It is undefined when γ1 is so large that the band is empty
enough for the discrete Gram to go indefinite.

## What the gap is not

Not a mass gap of an infinite-volume Hamiltonian.
Not BCS Δ(T). It is the ratio of the two smallest
eigenvalues of a finite positive matrix built from hats
at the zeros. Units: none.

## Practical cut

gap > 10² : quote N_eff and ell.
gap < 10 : do not say singlet (Δ⊗χ₅).
INDEF : do not say gap (Δ, ζ).

The one-set lower bound talks about ell = −log λ0, not
about λ1/λ0. The gap only certifies that λ0 is a
simple bottom, so ell is the depth of one mode.
