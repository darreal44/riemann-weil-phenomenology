# Effects of convexity on the hand bound

Convexity is the licence for the comparison, not a size.

    g''>0 tangent and supporting parabola lie below g.
    g''<0 chord and more-curved parabola (m≤inf g'') lie below g.
    g''=0 the two families coincide (inflection).

That is why the same code path works on the well and on the tail with
only the sign of m_i changed. Convexity does not set the gap: |g''| does
(`curvature-impact.md`). A long weakly convex rise moved less than a
short strongly convex well.

G_reg has the opposite pattern by s₀ (`Greg-curvature.md`): even cells
concave, odd cells convex. One cannot copy the g-mesh onto G_reg without
flipping the rule with s₀. g here is 2e^{−3y/2}−θ_v for one v, not
G_reg.
