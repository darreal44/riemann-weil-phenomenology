# C(χ) in detail

C = ell / (L|E|). C_Q from scan_s, C_G from the zero Gram (N=29).
Where both exist they agree to 5–8 % (χ₂₉, χ₁₇).

narrow side after μ=22.

## What moves C

- **Number of pieces of E.** At μ=11, E has 1–9 components and C_G
  is high whenever the desert dominates (χ₃ 0.46, χ₅ 0.37, χ₁₂ 0.45).
  At μ=50, 16–66 pieces, C has settled. Adding pieces dilutes C until
  Weyl self-similarity.
- **Not Imax/E.** That fraction falls with μ for everyone (more gaps
  enter E) while C plateaus. The ratio is not C.
- **Not mean piece length.** At μ=50 every character has mean piece
  ≈ 2.0. C still differs by a factor two.
- **Agreement Gram / Q.** The efficiency is a property of (Γ, L) in
  the window basis, not of the prime-side assembly.

## Transient

χ₁₇: C_Q = 0.21 → 0.19 → 0.11 and then flat. The low-μ points are
the same transient as s_loc climbing. Quote C only after the
plateau. χ₂₉ is already flat from μ=11 (narrow desert, no transient
in C). χ₅ is flat on 38–62.

## Reading

C(χ) is the asymptotic efficiency of E_L as a hiding set for one
mode of type log μ / 2. It freezes once the low-lying shape of Γ
(desert + first tight pairs) is a vanishing fraction of |E| and the
new gaps arrive at Weyl spacing. Two numerical values appear,
~0.09 and ~0.20, not a continuous function of γ₁. Naming them
would be another a,b.
