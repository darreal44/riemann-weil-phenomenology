χ₅, μ=3, A on V_N
(no primes):

    N    λ_min(A)    max |λ|(A⁻¹ Θ)
    2    −0.213      2.63
    3    −0.220      2.57
    5    −0.224      2.52

A is indefinite on the
2-plane already.
1/|w₂|≈2.04, and
max |λ|(A⁻¹Θ) > 2.04
on top of that — but
the ratio |θ|/A is not
even defined as a
positive-denominator
norm, because A takes
negative values.

So
    A_L − |w₂| |θ(log 2)|
cannot be a minorant
of Q on W_L: A_L itself
is not a minorant of
anything useful. Q’s
positivity on the
ladder comes from
−w₂ θ *cancelling the
negative mode of A*,
not from A dominating
T₂.

The missing line is a
joint form
A − w₂ Θ_{log 2}
on the whole W_L, i.e.
Q itself with only
prime 2. That is the
step, restated. C_*
does not shorten it.
