# Spectral properties of Q̂_L

On W_L the form
is bounded
(RH-free half).
There is a
bounded
self-adjoint
Q̂_L. The class
step is
spec(Q̂_L) ⊂ [0,∞),
i.e. c_L^* =
λ_min(Q̂_L) ≥ 0.

What we know
of the spectrum,
L=log 3:

- Finite
  compressions
  (hats, cosines):
  λ_min(S_N)>0
  and decreasing.
  χ₅: 0.044 at
  N=9. χ₈: H00
  =0.311. Does
  not prove the
  limit.

- Multiplier
  m_Q: spec-like
  picture on ℝ,
  inf m_Q =
  α_line/2 < 0.
  That is not
  spec(Q̂_L);
  W_L cannot
  feed every ρ.

- Schur block:
  H (head) has
  λ_min 0.053
  (χ₅), 0.257
  (χ₈), 0.070
  (χ₄ at h=20).
  Tail T has
  qmin ≳ 2–4
  on the
  diagonal.
  Coupling C
  is HS-small
  (‖C‖_F~0.4).
  Taken: χ₈, χ₄.
  χ₅: true-ish
  S_diag=+0.042,
  not certified.

- Q_∞ (no
  primes): one
  negative
  eigenvalue
  ~−0.074. T₂
  fills it on
  the ground
  state. The
  negative
  direction of
  A is not a
  negative
  direction of
  Q.

No complete
spectrum. No
Weyl law for
Q̂_L. The only
spectral number
the class needs
is the sign of
c_L^*.
