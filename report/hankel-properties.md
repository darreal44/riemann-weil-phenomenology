# Hankel matrix properties, as used

    Constant antidiagonals a_{n,m}=c_{n+m}.
    Rank finite ⇔ {c_k} is rational (Kronecker). 1/k is not.
    Norm Nehari: dist(φ,H^∞). Here π (½π after the ½ in Q).
    Compactness Hartman: φ ∈ H^∞+C. log is not. Tail s₁ does not fall.
    Positivity a Hankel of moments is PSD iff the measure is positive
    (Hamburg). 1/(n+m) is *not* a moment Hankel of a positive measure on
    [0,∞) in the Stieltjes sense that would make Off≥0 by itself (the
    kernel 1/(n+m) is PSD on ℓ² actually — Hilbert is a positive form).
    Off of Q is H plus a non-Hankel Θ term.

The PSD of 1/(n+m) does not make Q̂≥0: Q̂ has a diagonal and a Θ piece.
Not a new list. Not RH.
