# A + 4L/13 still drifts

v₀² (−L/2) = −4L/13
is the φ₀-diagonal piece.
The remainder is not a
constant:

    μ     A+4L/13    P+4L/13     Q
    6      0.283      0.189     0.093
   16      0.025      0.020     0.0055
   19     −0.002     −0.009     0.007
   29     −0.052     −0.061     0.009
   37     −0.072     −0.076     0.004

Both remainders cross 0
near μ=19 and keep going
negative: about −0.02 per
unit L. The measured
slope A/L → −0.328 is
steeper than −4/13 ≈
−0.307 by 0.02. That
extra is the other hats
and the slow drift of
A₀₀/L away from −1/2
(−0.499 at μ=16, not yet
the limit).

So −4L/13 is the right
*origin* of the leading
term, not the full
coefficient. Q is still
the difference of two
parallel drifts, not a
proven constant.
