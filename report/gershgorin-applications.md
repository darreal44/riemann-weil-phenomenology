when
the
matrix
is
diagonally
dominant
(R_i < |A_ii|)
or
has
few
nonzeros
per
row
(B₁).
Then
one
gets
invertibility
or
a
norm
bound
in
one
line.

Not

full
infinite
Hankel
or
for
Q_tail:
row
sums
diverge,
dominance
fails,
and
the
theorem
says
nothing
about
λ_min>0.

ovals
of
Cassini
(|z−A_ii||z−A_jj| ≤ R_i R_j)
are
tighter
disks
and
still
need
finite
R_i.
Same
obstruction
on
T_far.

lab
already
spent
that
bullet
on
B₁
(`notes/B1-bound.md`).

Gershgorin
to

reopens
