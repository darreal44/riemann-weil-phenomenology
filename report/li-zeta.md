# Li coefficients for ζ

    λ_n = ∑_ρ [1−(1−1/ρ)^n]

    λ_1 = ½(γ + 2 − log 4π) ≈ 0.02310

All published lists (n into the thousands and beyond) are positive. That
is compatible with RH and does not prove it (Bombieri– Lagarias: need
every n).

Under RH plus simple zeros, λ_n ∼ ½ (log n)² with oscillations from the
γ. A negative λ_N would be a disproof.

Relation to this repo: Li is Weil positivity for one sequence of
admissible tests (not compact support). Q̂_L is Weil positivity for the
PW window. Neither list has been computed here as λ_n(ζ). The ξ track
(`xi-track.md`) was Schur on q=1, not Li.

No harvest of λ_n planned: the values are a solved numerical problem
elsewhere, and a finite positive prefix does not move W_L.
