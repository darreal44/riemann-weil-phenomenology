# Laplace operator on the window — χ₂₉ μ=11

Hats φ_n = cos(ω_n y), ω_n = 2π n / L, are the
Neumann eigenfunctions of Δ = −d²/dy² on [0,L].
Eigenvalues: ω_n², ω_1≈2.62, ω_24≈62.9.

G is already written in that eigenbasis.

## How diagonal is Weil in the Laplace basis?

| | |

| nearest-neighbour rms | 0.50 |

G is a Jacobi-like perturbation of a multiplication
by a slow function of n. corr(diag, n)=0.70,
corr(diag, n²)=0.60 — not a polynomial in Δ.

## Ground state

|v₀|² lives in n=0,1,2 at 99.1 %.
The well is the infrared of Δ, not a high
Laplace mode. That is why Courant's 2-plane
span{e₁,e₂} (a mix of n=0 and n=1) can be
SPD while Q still has a well: the well is
mostly φ₀+φ₁, but the extra 1 % in n≥3 is
enough to drop λ₀ by a factor 10 relative
to that face.

## What this is not

Δ itself does not have the γ's as spectrum.
Commuting Q with Δ would force G diagonal;
the 3 % off-diagonal is the arithmetic
(primes + χ) mixing Laplace modes.
