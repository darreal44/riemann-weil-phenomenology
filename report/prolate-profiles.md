# Prolate spheroidal wave functions — profiles

S_c ψ = λ ψ on L²[−1,1],
c = ΩT/2. Even k even, odd k
odd (exact, the kernel is even).

## Two-mode window, c = π

| k | λ | parity | ψ(−1) | ψ(0) | ψ(1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.981 | even | 0.23 | 0.95 | 0.23 |
| 1 | 0.749 | odd | −0.75 | 0 | 0.75 |
| 2 | 0.243 | even | −1.35 | 0.65 | −1.35 |

k=0 hump in the middle,
still O(1) at the edge.
k=1 one node at 0.
k=2 already leaking
(λ=0.24). This *is* N_eff≈2.

## Desert windows

c=14.7 (μ=8) and c=21.8 (μ=22):
the first three λ equal 1.000
to five digits. Profiles
vanish at ±1 and oscillate
in the bulk — the classical
“all in the passband” regime.
No well: every low mode is
perfectly concentrated.

## Against Q

Q's (v₀, v₁) copy the
parity of (ψ₀, ψ₁) at
c=π, not the shapes at
c_desert. Depths of Q
(ℓ=14…39) have no analogue
in λ_k(c): those are in
(0,1), and −ln(1−λ₀) is
4 at c=π, 26 at c=14.7,
while Q's extra depth is
the A−P cancel inside the
even combination, not a
larger time-bandwidth
product.

A plot of ψ₀(x) at c=π
is the function whose
hat-coefficients would
have to match v₀ if Q
were S_c. They do not
(report/slepian-pairs.md):
v₀ mixes φ₀ and φ₁,
ψ₀ of the desert is φ₀.
