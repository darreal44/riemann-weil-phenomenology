# LPS construction, as a pointer

Lubotzky–Phillips–Sarnak: vertices are PGL₂(F_q) or a congruence
quotient of an order in the Hamilton quaternions ramified at ∞ and one
odd prime. Edges come from a fixed set of p+1 integral quaternions of
norm p (p ≡ 1 mod 4 typically). The adjacency eigenvalues are read from
Hecke eigenvalues of weight-2 forms; Ramanujan–Petersson gives the 2√p
bound.

That is all the detail this lab needs. It does not produce a test
function in W_L and does not replace Guinand–Weil.

Not a campaign.
