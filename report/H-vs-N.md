# Impact of N on H — χ₂₉ Gram

H = Q[0:3,0:3] is the Gram of hats
n=0,1,2 only. Extra hats do not
enter those inner products.

Measured: H00, H11, H22, λ_min(H)
are **bit-identical** from N=6 to 48
at every μ.

What N does change:

| μ | N=6 λ₀(Q) | N=24 λ₀(Q) | N=48 λ₀(Q) | λ_min(T) 6→48 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | 0.308 | 0.298 | 0.298 | 2.92 → 2.59 |
| 22 | 0.0072 | 0.0041 | 0.0040 | 3.12 → 1.82 |
| 38 | 1e-4 | 8e-6 | 8e-6 | 3.03 → 0.99 |

λ₀(Q) has converged by N=16–24.
T's floor keeps sagging a little
as high hats are added; that is
the only N-dependence of Schur.

The collapse of H with μ is a
property of (γ, L), not of the
window dimension.
