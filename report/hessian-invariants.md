# Hessian invariants

g(y) is a function of one variable: its Hessian is g''(y), already the
driver of the mesh. There are no classical Hessian invariants (of a
ternary cubic) hiding in that number.

A(v) on the 3-plane is a quadratic form. Its Hessian is the constant
matrix 2A (or 2Q on the identified block). The invariants of that
Hessian are the eigenvalues of H_2 already scored (λ_min, det, trace).
They do not control whether g=2e^{−3y/2}−θ_v has a convex well on [0,1]:
that is a property of the *integrand*, not of the quadratic form as a
matrix.

#vscan showed the well dies for e₀ and survives for the rational
witness. That split is not an invariant of Hess A. It is a shape of
θ_v(y).
