# Spectral asymptotics

Weyl
is
the
leading
term
of
the
eigenvalue
counting
function.
The
next
term
(surface,
or
oscillatory
from
periodic
orbits
in
the
trace
formula)
is
what
people
call
spectral
asymptotics
beyond
Weyl.

For
Q̂_L
none
of
that
is
written.
The
only
asymptotics
in
hand
are:

- λ_1(H_n)
  ↓
  in
  n
  by
  ~30 %
  from
  16
  to
  48
  at
  fixed
  L
  (#74).
- λ_1(H₁₆)
  ↓
  like
  10^{−c μ}
  on
  (5,8]
  (#75),
  not
  a
  Weyl
  remainder.
- Q_nn
  = O(1)
  in
  the
  bulk
  at
  L=log 3.

A
two-term
law
would
need
the
length
of
the
set
{m_Q ≤ λ}
and
a
boundary
correction
from
the
ends
of
the
window
±L.
Not
computed.

Trace
formula
for
Tr
f(Q̂_L)
would
be
circular:
Q̂
is
already
the
explicit
formula.

No
campaign.
