    H₀₁ = A(φ₀,φ₁) − w₂ θ₀₁(log 2)

θ₀₁(log 2) from
`theta_hat(0,1,·)`:

    −2 sin(ω₁ y)/(√2 π)  at y=log 2
    = +0.3299

w₂(χ₅)=w₂(χ₃)=−0.490
⇒ −w₂ θ₀₁ = **+0.1617**

increment read
off the table
(0.485−0.324,
0.480−0.318).
Closed form,
not a fit.

diagonal, smaller
det, smaller λ_H.

increases H₀₀
(`atom-on-H00.md`).

off-diagonal
growth outruns
the diagonal

does not get
the same lift
(1.12 vs 1.63).

χ(2)=0: this
term is absent.

archimedean
