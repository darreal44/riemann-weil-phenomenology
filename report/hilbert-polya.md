# Hilbert–Pólya

Conjecture: there is a self-adjoint operator H on a Hilbert space whose
eigenvalues are the γ (or 1/2+iγ). RH would follow from
self-adjointness.

Candidates (none a theorem): Berry–Keating xp, Connes’ adelic space,
various transfer operators. GUE statistics are the empirical hint
(`rmt-quantum-chaos.md`).

Q̂_L is not that H. Its eigenvalues are numbers attached to a window of
test functions, not the γ. A negative eigenvalue of the identified Q̂
would be a bad test function (a disproof of GRH for that θ), not a
complex γ.

Hilbert’s inequality (π on the Hankel tail) is a different Hilbert
(`hilbert-inequality.md`).

No campaign to build H. The lab already has the quadratic form that
would be positive if RH held; it does not have the spectral realisation
of the zeros.
