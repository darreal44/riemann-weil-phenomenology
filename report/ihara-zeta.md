# Ihara zeta

For a finite connected graph X,

    ζ_X(u) = ∏_{[C]} (1 − u^{ℓ(C)})^{−1}

over primitive closed geodesics. Bass–Ihara: ζ_X(u)^{−1} = (1−u²)^{χ}
det(I − A u + Q u²) with Q=D−I. Poles are read from the spectrum of A.

X Ramanujan ⇔ ζ_X has no poles in q^{−1/2} < |u| < q^{1/2} (the graph
RH). That equivalence is the definition of Ramanujan rewritten in
u=q^{−s}.

Different from ξ(s) and from Q(θ). No test function, no explicit formula
of Guinand–Weil type on W_L. Hashimoto and Bass determinants do not
bound θ(y).

Not a campaign.
