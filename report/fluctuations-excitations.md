# Fluctuations, vibrations, excited states

χ₂₉ and χ₁₇ at μ=22, Gram N=33.

## Fluctuations of the singlet

v0 is odd on one pair (61 % / 43 %) but the mass entropy is
S = 2.34 and 2.88 nats — equivalent to 10 and 18 zeros. That tail
is the fluctuation / screening cloud. It is not a second mode; it
is v0 leaking onto the rest of Γ.

## Vibrations

j ≥ 2 have k̄ = 6–22 and λ = O(1). They are higher harmonics of the
window (spatial nodes), even on their pairs. They do not bind.
Ladder in k̄, not in ell.

## Excited states of the impurity

χ₁₇ j=1,2 stay *odd* on a second pair (10.62–11.98), λ = 0.17 and
0.94. That is an excited odd combination, still not isolated
(λ₁/λ₀ = 5×10⁴ for j=0 vs this pair). χ₂₉ has no odd excited state
in the first ten: j=1 is even on the desert. The singlet has no
cheap odd excitation on the *same* pair — that would have been a
triplet on the charged pair, and it is missing.

Quantum fluctuations of the singlet are the 40–60 % tail, not a
soft mode.
