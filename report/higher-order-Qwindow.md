# Higher-order corrections to Q_window

Relative to A − ∑_{p<μ} w_p θ(log p).

**p^k.** At μ=5 only 2². ΔH₀₀=−0.096 on χ₃ (`pk-on-4plane.md`). Largest
correction we have a number for. Sign against the tiny well.

**T₅ just above 5.** O(ε) in θ, +0.017 on χ₃ H₀₀ at μ=5.1. Next order in
ε is the shape of θ away from the edge, already in `theta_hat`.

**Towers 3^k, 5^k.** 3²=9>5, 5²=25>5: none at this μ.

**Archimedean CST vs Γ.** The constant log(q/π)−γ −log(1−e^{−2L}) is a
model of the Γ-factor pairing, not the classical term term-by-term.
Error not bounded in this repo. Could be larger than 10⁻⁴.

**Gauss of D₂.** 16 vs 32 vs 64 panels: 1e-8 on χ₃ H (#67). Not the
sign.

**‖Θ‖ 1 vs 2.** At μ=5, log 2 < L/2, the scripts use 2. That is a bound
on the tail operator, not a correction to H₄ (H₄ is computed from θ_hat,
exact on the 4-plane).

The correction that can change the *meaning* of μ* is p^k plus the true
Γ, not more Gauss panels.
